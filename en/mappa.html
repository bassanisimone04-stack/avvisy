    How to read the map

    • Real filming location

      The scene was actually shot at this venue.

    • Film-related

      A real venue that was not filmed in the movie: it is near the set or tied to the phenomenon the film portrays.

    • Closed

      Historic venues that have closed stay listed for their film history; use the filter to hide them.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I use the venue map? +

    Zoom and drag the map, then click a marker to see the venue's name, city, type and the films it is linked to. "Discover more" opens the full venue page. Use the filters above the map to narrow down by category, region or open venues only.

    How many venues are on the map? +

    The map shows 72 venues in 45 cities across 18 regions, linked to 41 films and TV shows. It updates automatically every time we add a venue.

    What is the difference between "Real location" and "Film-related"? +

    "Real location" means the scene was actually shot there. "Film-related" means a real venue that was not filmed in the movie (for example near the set, or tied to the phenomenon the film portrays). We always say so on the venue page.

    Are the markers exact? +

    For some venues the marker position is approximate. Before you travel, always check the written address on the venue page, and the opening hours.

    Are there closed venues on the map? +

    Yes: a few historic venues that have closed stay published with a "closed" status because of their film history. Tick "Open venues only" to hide them.

    Do I need to book? +

    It's advisable, especially for small venues and starred restaurants. Check the venue's official website for hours and availability, which can change.

    How can I plan a trip following the films? +

    Pick a city page to see all its film venues together, read the blog itineraries, or download the PDF guide with four ready-made itineraries.

    How do I report a missing venue or a mistake? +

    Use the "Add a venue" page. We only publish venues with a verifiable source linking them to a real film or TV show.
