The Devil Wears Prada 2 Filming Locations: Milan

Where The Devil Wears Prada 2 was filmed in Milan: Palazzo Parigi Hotel and Caffè Parigi, the cast's base during the 2025 shoot.

Twenty years after winning over audiences, The Devil Wears Prada returns with a sequel that puts Andy Sachs face to face with Miranda Priestly again — this time as a media executive, not an assistant. And Italy is back too: the summer 2025 shoot brought the cast into the heart of Milan’s fashion world, with a tourism effect still being measured today.

Palazzo Parigi, the cast’s base

For several weeks, Caffè Parigi, the café-bistrot of the Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa between Brera and the Fashion Quadrilateral, became the production’s Milan base. Scenes were shot in the hotel lobby, at Caffè Parigi, and in the building’s exterior spaces — right during Milan Fashion Week, the perfect backdrop for a film built around fashion.

A measurable tourism boom

This isn’t just a detail for film buffs: according to Confcommercio Milano data (Cities Analytics), reported by Variety and Il Sole 24 Ore in August 2026, the film’s locations saw an 18% rise in international visitors in summer 2026, peaking at 28% around Palazzo Clerici. A rare case where a film’s tourism impact can be measured in precise numbers, months after release.

Breakfast or aperitivo at Caffè Parigi

Caffè Parigi is open to the public every day, with breakfast, light lunches and evening aperitivo in an elegant setting — the same one the cast spent weeks in during filming. It’s a convenient stop for anyone visiting Brera or the Fashion Quadrilateral, minutes from the city’s most famous shopping streets.

In short

The Devil Wears Prada 2 put international attention back on Milan as a fashion capital — and Palazzo Parigi is still the place to breathe the same atmosphere as the set. Discover more films and shows shot in Italy, or the full list of movie restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

▸ Was The Devil Wears Prada 2 really filmed in Milan?

Yes. For several weeks in summer 2025, the cast and crew stayed at the Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa, where scenes were shot in the lobby, at Caffè Parigi, and in the hotel's exterior spaces, during Fashion Week.

▸ Did the film bring more tourists to Milan?

Yes: Confcommercio Milano data, reported by Variety and Il Sole 24 Ore (August 2026), shows an 18% rise in international visitors in summer 2026 at the film's locations, peaking at +28% around Palazzo Clerici.

▸ Can you visit Caffè Parigi?

Yes, it's open to the public inside a five-star hotel: breakfast, aperitivo and table service every day.

▸ Who stars in The Devil Wears Prada 2?

Meryl Streep returns as Miranda Priestly, Anne Hathaway as Andy Sachs and Emily Blunt as Emily Charlton, twenty years after the first film, directed by David Frankel.
