Tucci in Le Marche: Uliassi in Senigallia

Where Stanley Tucci ate in Le Marche in Tucci in Italy: Uliassi in Senigallia, the three-Michelin-star restaurant born in 1990 as a beach kiosk.

Season two of Tucci in Italy, the National Geographic series released in May 2026, devotes its third episode to Le Marche. It’s a different series from Searching for Italy, which was CNN’s: the host is still Stanley Tucci, but the production is another. The restaurant we can point to with a verified address is Uliassi.

Uliassi, in Senigallia

Uliassi is at Banchina di Levante 6, in Senigallia. It is a three-Michelin-star restaurant (Guide Italia 2026), the only three-star in Le Marche, run by siblings Mauro (kitchen) and Catia (front of house) Uliassi. It began in 1990 as a family-run place on the beach and, after three renovations over twenty years, reached its present level.

Local press (Corriere Adriatico, Centropagina) reported Tucci’s visit to the restaurant, which took place about two years before the season aired.

What we haven’t listed

In the episode Tucci also visits Campofilone, home of maccheroncini di Campofilone IGP, at the Spinosi family workshop, where pasta is made with flour and eggs as it has been for generations. It is an artisan workshop, not a restaurant, so we haven’t listed it.

Frequently asked questions

▸ Where did Stanley Tucci eat in Le Marche?

At Uliassi in Senigallia, Mauro and Catia Uliassi's three-Michelin-star restaurant, in the Marche episode of Tucci in Italy.

▸ Is Tucci in Italy the same series as Searching for Italy?

No: Searching for Italy was a CNN series (2021-2022), while Tucci in Italy is a National Geographic series that premiered in 2025.

▸ Where is Uliassi?

At Banchina di Levante 6, in Senigallia, in the province of Ancona, on the sea.

▸ How did Uliassi begin?

In 1990 as a family-run beach place: after three renovations over twenty years it earned three Michelin stars. Mauro Uliassi is in the kitchen, his sister Catia front of house.

▸ When was the episode filmed?

Local press reports that Tucci's visit to Uliassi took place about two years before season two aired in May 2026.
