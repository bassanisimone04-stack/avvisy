The Hand of God Filming Locations in Naples

Paolo Sorrentino's Netflix film is shot between real Naples streets and Villa Giusso, an agriturismo on the Sorrentine Peninsula.

With The Hand of God, Paolo Sorrentino told the story of his own Naples — his teenage years and a family tragedy that shaped his life — in a largely autobiographical film, winner of the Silver Lion in Venice and an Oscar nominee.

Sorrentino’s Vomero

The Schisa family home is filmed on Via San Domenico, in the Vomero district, just one floor from the director’s real childhood home. Among the other real locations: the Galleria Umberto I, where Fabietto meets director Antonio Capuano, Piazza del Plebiscito, the Gulf of Naples, and the Maradona stadium.

Villa Giusso, the family lunch

One of the film’s most intense scenes — the big family lunch — is shot at Villa Giusso, an agriturismo set in a 17th-century former monastery in the hills above Vico Equense, on the Sorrentine Peninsula, at about 400 metres altitude overlooking the Gulf of Naples. It’s a genuinely public venue, with a kitchen built around the estate’s own produce.

In short

The Hand of God is an intimate, autobiographical portrait by Sorrentino, shot between real Naples streets and the Sorrentine countryside. Villa Giusso is still there, open to visitors, for anyone who wants to experience the same landscape as the film. Frequently asked questions

▸ Was The Hand of God really filmed in Naples?

Yes: among the real locations, the Schisa family home is filmed on Via San Domenico, in the Vomero district, one floor from Paolo Sorrentino's real childhood home. Piazza del Plebiscito and the Galleria Umberto I also appear.

▸ Where was the family lunch scene filmed?

At Villa Giusso, an agriturismo set in a 17th-century former monastery in Vico Equense, on the Sorrentine Peninsula, overlooking the Gulf of Naples.

▸ Is The Hand of God a true story?

Yes, it's largely autobiographical: it tells the story of Paolo Sorrentino's adolescence in Naples and the tragedy that struck his family. It won the Silver Lion in Venice and was nominated for the Best International Feature Oscar.
