TV Shows Filmed in Italy: The Real Locations

From The White Lotus to Il Re, from Ripley to Rocco Schiavone: the international TV series genuinely filmed in Italy, with verified locations.

Television has become one of the most powerful engines of film tourism in Italy. From Sky productions to Netflix and HBO, more and more international shows are choosing Italian cities and villages as genuine sets — not just backdrops, but places you can actually go and visit.

Where The White Lotus was filmed: Taormina, Sicily

Season 2 of the HBO series made Taormina one of the most searched destinations of 2022-2023. Discover the real locations in our article on The White Lotus and the real Sicily.

Ripley — Amalfi Coast

The Netflix miniseries, winner of two Emmys, chose Atrani to recreate the early-1960s Amalfi Coast (the series is set in 1961), from Patricia Highsmith’s novel. Details in our deep dive on Ripley.

Inspector Montalbano — Ragusa and Punta Secca

For over twenty years, the Rai series based on Camilleri’s novels has made Punta Secca, Scicli and Ragusa Ibla famous. The inspector is also a food lover: discover where Montalbano ate.

My Brilliant Friend — Naples

The HBO/Rai series based on Elena Ferrante’s novels mixes a built set in Marcianise with real Naples streets, including the historic Galleria Principe. Read the real Naples behind the set.

Il Re — Trieste

The Sky Original series starring Luca Zingaretti filmed across Rome, Turin and Trieste, where it also used the interiors of the Antico Caffè San Marco. Discover Il Re in Trieste.

Rocco Schiavone — Aosta

Based on Antonio Manzini’s novels, the Rai series filmed in Aosta, where a historic café was reopened just for the shoot. Read the story of Caffè Nazionale.

Succession — Tuscany

The season 3 finale wedding of the HBO series about the Roy family is shot between Villa La Foce and Villa Cetinale, in the Val d’Orcia. Discover the real Tuscany of Succession.

Master of None — Modena

Season 2 of the Netflix series created by Aziz Ansari opens in Modena, between a fresh pasta lesson and a dinner at Osteria Francescana. Read Modena and Osteria Francescana.

Blocco 181 – Gangs of Milano — Turin

The Sky series created by Salmo is set in Milan but was filmed almost entirely in Turin: Ristorante Del Cambio is among the more than a hundred Piedmont locations used for season 2. Discover Blocco 181 in Turin.

The Red Door — Trieste

The Rai noir starring Lino Guanciale and Gabriella Pession filmed for three seasons in Trieste, with the Antico Caffè San Marco among its recurring locations. Discover The Red Door and Caffè San Marco.

Commissioner Ricciardi — Naples

Set in 1930s Naples, the Rai series starring Lino Guanciale filmed in authentic city locations, including the Gran Caffè Gambrinus, transformed into the period café the commissioner visits. Discover the real Gambrinus.

Don Matteo — Gubbio, Umbria

For many seasons, the longest-running Italian crime-comedy series used Gubbio as its fixed set: Caffè Ducale, on Piazza Grande, is still the café where Don Matteo and Sergeant Cecchini play their chess games. Discover Don Matteo in Gubbio.

Anthony Bourdain: No Reservations — Trastevere, Rome

In the Rome episode of the Travel Channel culinary travel series, Anthony Bourdain finds his favorite cacio e pepe at Ristorante Roma Sparita, initially keeping its name secret. Discover Trastevere’s secret trattoria.

Màkari — Scopello, Sicily

In the Rai series starring Claudio Gioè, Marilù’s restaurant is a set built inside the old Tonnara di Scopello, overlooking the sea stacks between Castellammare del Golfo and San Vito Lo Capo. Discover the real tonnara.

In short

From the Sicily of The White Lotus to the Aosta Valley of Rocco Schiavone, international TV series keep choosing Italy as their set — and leaving behind real locations open to the public. Frequently asked questions

▸ Which famous TV shows were filmed in Italy?

The White Lotus (Sicily), Inspector Montalbano (Sicily), My Brilliant Friend (Naples), Ripley (Amalfi Coast), Il Re (Trieste), Rocco Schiavone (Aosta) and Succession, partly filmed in Tuscany.

▸ Why do more and more international shows choose Italy?

For the variety of landscapes, intact historic architecture, and the support of regional film commissions, which provide locations, permits and often tax incentives to productions.

▸ Can you actually visit these TV show locations?

Many of them, yes: on CineFood Italia we only publish venues (bars, restaurants, cafés) verified as real filming locations, with an address and a source.

▸ Which show has driven the most tourism to Italy recently?

The White Lotus, whose second season was set in Taormina in 2022, made Taormina one of the most searched destinations of 2022-2023.
