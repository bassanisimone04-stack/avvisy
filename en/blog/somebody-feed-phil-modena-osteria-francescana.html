Somebody Feed Phil in Modena: Osteria Francescana

Where Phil Rosenthal ate in Modena in Somebody Feed Phil: Osteria Francescana, Bar Tiffany for gnocco fritto, La Consorteria 1966 and Mercato Albinelli.

The Somebody Feed Phil episode devoted to Venice (season 2, episode 1) also includes Modena. Phil Rosenthal arrives with guide Caterina Schenetti and goes from Mercato Albinelli to the venues that tell Emilian cooking. Sources are the host’s official website, Phil Rosenthal World, and city guides.

Osteria Francescana

The main moment is dinner at Osteria Francescana, Massimo Bottura’s three-Michelin-star restaurant at Via Stella 22. Phil dines in the kitchen on a tasting menu that includes five ages of Parmigiano. The same restaurant also appears in Master of None.

Bar Tiffany: gnocco fritto

With Caterina, Phil tries gnocco fritto at Bar Tiffany, on Via Canalino 58: dough fried in lard, to eat with cured meats.

Balsamic vinegar and the market

La Consorteria 1966, at Piazza Mazzini 9, is a shop devoted to Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena PDO: Phil tastes vinegars aged 12 and 25 years. Mercato Albinelli is the city centre’s covered market. They are a shop and a market, not venues with tables, and we mention them without listing them.

What to eat in Modena, like Phil

Gnocco fritto, tortellini, traditional balsamic vinegar and Parmigiano Reggiano. For the Venice part of the same episode read Somebody Feed Phil in Venice; for another real Modena venue, Hosteria Giusti.
