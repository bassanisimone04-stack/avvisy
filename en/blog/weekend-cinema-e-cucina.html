A Weekend of Cinema and Good Food in Italy

A practical guide for a two-day trip in the footsteps of Italian films, between locations, restaurants and local dishes.

A “cinematic” weekend is the perfect way to combine travel, cinema and food. Here is how to plan it in a few steps, with some ready-made ideas to start from.

1. Choose the film (or the city)

Start from a title you love or a city you want to visit. In our Films section you’ll find the works with their linked venues; in the city pages (like Rome) you’ll find all the sets of a single destination. For a first trip, compact villages like Savoca or Crema are easier to explore on foot than a big city.

2. Build the itinerary

Alternate locations and food stops. Use the map to see which venues are close to each other and avoid too much travelling. A good pace is two or three stops a day: better to enjoy a few places well than cram in too many.

3. Book in advance

The most famous restaurants fill up fast, especially in high season (summer, long weekends, holidays). Check the “booking” field on the venue pages and always confirm opening hours and closing days — many historic venues close one day a week.

4. Tell real sets from film-related venues

Not all venues are real sets: some are only film-related, meaning near a set but not filmed there. They are useful stops, but it’s nice to know what to expect — here’s the difference explained in detail.

5. Leave room for the unexpected

Film villages are small and surprising: treat yourself to an extra stroll, a coffee in the square, an improvised granita. Often it’s the unplanned stop that becomes the best memory of the trip.

Frequently asked questions

▸ How do you plan a film-tourism weekend in Italy?

Choose a film and its city, identify the connected locations and restaurants and build a two-day itinerary.

▸ Which destinations for a cinema-and-food weekend?

Rome, Montalbano's Sicily, the Cilento of Welcome to the South or the Crema of Call Me by Your Name.

▸ Do you need to book in advance?

Yes, especially for the most famous restaurants and in high season.

▸ Which cinema-and-food weekends would you recommend to start?

Savoca and Sicily's Ionian coast (The Godfather), Castellabate and Cilento (Welcome to the South), or Crema (Call Me by Your Name): three destinations easily covered in a weekend, each with very different locations and cuisine.
