Amalfi Coast Filming Locations: Ripley, Equalizer

The real Amalfi Coast filming locations: Atrani in Netflix's Ripley and in The Equalizer 3, Positano in Hotel Costiera.

Few coastlines in the world have the cinematic density of the Amalfi Coast. In recent years two major international productions have chosen the same small village, Atrani, to tell very different stories — proof that this stretch of coast still draws Hollywood as much as it did in the Dolce Vita years.

Ripley (Netflix, 2024): Atrani as the early-1960s Amalfi Coast

Netflix’s Ripley, starring Andrew Scott and winner of two 2024 Emmys, chose Atrani to recreate the early-1960s Amalfi Coast (the series is set in 1961) from Patricia Highsmith’s novel. It’s a different adaptation from The Talented Mr. Ripley (1999) with Matt Damon, shot instead on Ischia, Procida, in Rome and Venice: same book, different settings.

The Equalizer 3 (2023): Atrani becomes “Altamonte”

A year earlier, Antoine Fuqua had chosen the same village for The Equalizer 3, starring Denzel Washington. In the film Atrani is disguised as a fictional town, “Altamonte”: the production replaced the signs on almost every shop around the square, turning the real Ristorante Savò into the “Aminah’s Cafe” where the lead orders his tea every day.

Where the films were shot on the coast: why Atrani specifically

At barely 0.12 km² (about 12 hectares), Atrani is Italy’s smallest municipality by area: one little square, covered alleys, narrow staircases between the houses. It’s a few minutes’ walk from Amalfi, but far quieter — one of the reasons two different productions chose it within the space of a single year, over the better-known and busier Positano and Amalfi.

Positano: Hotel Costiera

A little further west, Positano stars in the series Hotel Costiera (2025): read the details in our article on Villa Treville and the real Positano.

An itinerary along the coast

Atrani, Amalfi and Positano follow one another along the SS163, the classic scenic coast road: all three can be visited in a single day, by car or on the SITA buses, though summer traffic can stretch travel times considerably. Atrani in particular has no parking in its old town, which is entirely pedestrian.

In short

From the early-1960s Amalfi Coast of Ripley to the contemporary thriller of The Equalizer 3, the same small village has lent its face to two opposite stories. Discover more films and shows shot in Italy, or the full list of movie restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

▸ Which films were shot on the Amalfi Coast?

Ripley (Netflix, 2024) and The Equalizer 3 (2023) were both filmed mainly in Atrani; the series Hotel Costiera (2025) is set in Positano.

▸ Why does Atrani keep coming up as a filming location?

It's Italy's smallest municipality by area, with a compact little square and vertical architecture that works well on camera, and it's quieter than nearby Amalfi and Positano.

▸ Does Denzel Washington's café in The Equalizer 3 really exist?

Yes: it's Ristorante Savò in Atrani, dressed by the production as the film's "Aminah's Cafe". The venue tells the story on its own official website.

▸ What's the difference between Netflix's Ripley and The Talented Mr. Ripley (1999)?

They're two different adaptations of the same Patricia Highsmith novel: the 1999 film with Matt Damon is shot on Ischia, Procida, in Rome and Venice, while the 2024 Netflix series with Andrew Scott uses Atrani and the Amalfi Coast.
