Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy

Directed by Stanley Tucci

A CNN food-travel series: actor Stanley Tucci explores Italian cuisine region by region, from Naples to Rome, Emilia-Romagna to Sicily, through trattorias, pizzerias and great restaurants. Winner of multiple Emmys for outstanding hosted non-fiction series.

Frequently asked questions

Where did Stanley Tucci eat in Searching for Italy?+

Among the venues listed by CNN: La Reginella in Rome's Jewish Ghetto (carciofi alla giudia), Enzo Coccia's pizzeria La Notizia in Naples, I Pupi in Bagheria and Mè Cumpari Turiddu in Catania (Sicily), Osteria del Cinghiale Bianco in Florence, Ratanà and Osteria del Treno in Milan, All'Arco, Villa 600, Venissa and Orient Experience in Venice, Christian e Manuel in Vercelli, Antichi Sapori, Bere Vecchie and Vitantonio Lombardo (Puglia and Matera), Alla Via di Mezzo (Umbria), Mabrouk, Luigi Pomata and Fradis Minoris (Sardinia), Osteria della Cipolla Rossa (Calabria), Cracco Portofino and Il Genovese (Liguria), Lo Scoglio in Marina del Cantone (spaghetti alla Nerano) and Casa Artusi in Forlimpopoli.

Who produces Searching for Italy?+

It's a CNN Original Series, airing since February 14, 2021 across two seasons, hosted by actor Stanley Tucci, whose family comes from Calabria.

Are the places Tucci visited still open?+

The ones listed on CineFood Italia are: we've verified each one. Hours and availability can still change, so check before you go.
