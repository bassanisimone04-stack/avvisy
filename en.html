    Frequently asked questions

    How the directory works and how to use it to plan a trip.

    What is CineFood Italia? +

    A directory of the real Italian restaurants, bars and cafés seen in films and TV shows: 72 venues in 45 cities, linked to 43 films and series, each with its address, the scene and a link to the venue.

    Where did Julia Roberts eat pizza in Eat Pray Love? +

    At L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele, on Via Cesare Sersale in Naples. The full story is in the article on the Eat Pray Love pizza place.

    Are all these venues really film locations? +

    Most are: the scene was actually shot there. A few are marked "film-related", meaning a real venue that was not filmed in the movie but sits next to the set or is tied to the story. We always say which is which on the venue page and on the map.

    How do you check the information? +

    Every venue-film pairing needs a verifiable source: the venue's own website, a regional film commission such as Italy for Movies, or recognised national press. If two sources disagree, we leave the pairing out.

    Can I plan a trip with the site? +

    Yes. Start from the map to see what is near you, open a city page to get all its venues together, or read the blog itineraries, film by film.

    Is there a guide to download? +

    Yes: a PDF guide with every venue by region plus four ready-made itineraries, handy offline.

    Do I have to book? +

    It's advisable, especially for small venues and starred restaurants. Opening hours and availability change, so check the venue's official website before you travel.

    My venue was in a film: how do I add it? +

    Use the Add a venue page. We publish only pairings we can verify with a source.
