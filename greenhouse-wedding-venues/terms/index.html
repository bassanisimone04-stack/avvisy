What this site is

GreenhouseVenues is a free, editorial directory of greenhouse, glasshouse and conservatory wedding venues. Listings are compiled from each venue’s own website, as described on how we source and verify listings. Using this site does not create any agreement between you and us beyond these terms.

This is not a booking platform

We do not take bookings, payments, deposits or commissions, and we are not a party to any contract between you and a venue. Every listing links to the venue’s own events page; availability, pricing and terms come from the venue directly, and only the venue can confirm them.

We source every fact we publish from a venue’s own website and mark anything unconfirmed as a dash rather than a guess — see our sourcing rules.
