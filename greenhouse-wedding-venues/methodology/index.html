Editorial standards

How we source and verify listings

A directory is only worth reading if you know where its facts come from. Here is exactly where ours do, and what we do when we do not know something.

The sourcing rule

A field is filled in only when it is stated on the venue’s own website. Not on a listing marketplace, not on a photographer’s blog, not from a review aggregator. Those sources go stale, disagree with each other, and are how directories end up telling couples something untrue about a real business.

Every listing links to the venue’s own events page. That link is the point of the listing: we are a shortlist and a set of comparable descriptions, not a booking system.

What a dash means

Where you see a dash or “not confirmed”, we have not verified that detail at the source. It does not mean the room is small, cheap, unavailable or unsuitable. It means we do not know yet, and would rather say so.

Right now that applies to most capacity, layout and price fields: 20 of 38 listings have a confirmed capacity and 0 of 38 a confirmed price range. The capacity, blend and price filters only match confirmed listings, which is why they currently return little or nothing. We would rather ship an honest empty filter than a populated dishonest one.

What we do not do

  • No paid placements. No venue pays to be listed or to rank, and we take no commission on bookings.
  • No invented ratings. We hold no first-party reviews, so we publish no star ratings and no aggregateRating in our structured data. A fabricated rating is both a Google spam-policy violation and a lie about someone’s business.
  • No scraped prices. If a venue does not publish a rate, we do not estimate one.
  • No borrowed photography. The images on this site are generated decorative patterns, marked as such. Venue photographs belong to the venues and their photographers.

Corrections and removals

If you work at a listed venue and something here is wrong or out of date, tell us and we will correct it. If you would rather not be listed at all, tell us and we will remove the listing — no negotiation, no upsell.

Suggesting a venue

Send the venue’s own events page. We check that the venue exists, hosts weddings, and has glazed space, then add it with whatever its site confirms.

Submits as a Netlify Form if this site is deployed on Netlify — entries land in your site’s Forms dashboard, no backend to run yourself. Deploying elsewhere? Point this form at your own handler instead. Prefer email? Write to hello@greenhousevenues.com directly — a placeholder address until the domain is live.
