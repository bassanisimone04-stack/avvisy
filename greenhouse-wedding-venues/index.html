Greenhouse wedding FAQs

The five questions couples send us most. Marked up as FAQPage schema so they can win the answer box.

How much does a greenhouse wedding venue cost?

Site fees for greenhouse and conservatory wedding venues in the US typically run $4,000–$22,000, with a national median around $9,800 for a Saturday in peak season. Botanical garden venues sit at the lower end because catering is usually open; private restored glasshouses sit at the top. Our greenhouse wedding cost guide breaks down every line item.

Are greenhouse wedding venues rain-proof?

Fully glazed venues are genuinely rain-proof — the ceremony, dinner and dancing all happen under a solid roof, so there is no weather call to make and no tent to hold. Hybrid venues keep the reception under glass and the ceremony in the garden, so you still get a same-day plan B. Filter for “Fully enclosed” to see only the all-weather rooms.

How hot does a glasshouse get in summer?

It depends entirely on the ventilation spec, not the climate. A glasshouse with roof vents, side louvers and shade cloth stays within 3–5°F of outside; one without can run 20°F hotter by 4pm. Every venue page on this directory lists the cooling system, so you can compare an Austin glasshouse against a Napa one honestly.

What is the smallest greenhouse wedding venue I can book?

Plenty of propagation houses and fern rooms seat 20–50 guests, and many release weekday dates at a fraction of the weekend fee. Use the “Under 50 — micro-wedding” capacity filter to see every small greenhouse wedding venue in the directory.

Can we get married in a greenhouse in Italy?

Yes — historic limonaie (lemon houses) and estate serre on Lake Como, in Tuscany and around Lake Garda host destination weddings, and several are licensed for legally binding civil ceremonies on site. See our Italy destination collection.
