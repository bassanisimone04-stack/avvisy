What counts as a greenhouse wedding venue

Six quite different buildings share the label. A working greenhouse is a growing structure released for events. A conservatory is a glazed room attached to a building, historically for overwintering tender plants. An orangery is its masonry cousin: solid piers, tall arched glazing, much easier to heat. A palm house is the tall domed Victorian type inside public gardens, an atrium its modern steel-and-glass equivalent, and a limonaia the Italian lemon house built to overwinter citrus.

The label describes the architecture. What decides your day is whether the room is fully enclosed and how it is ventilated — so ask those two questions of every venue, whatever it calls itself.

Frequently asked

How do I find glasshouse wedding venues near me?

Filter by state and venue type, then follow the link on each listing through to the venue’s own events page for current availability and pricing. Search also matches city names, so typing your nearest large city is often the fastest route.

What counts as conservatory wedding venues?

Strictly, a conservatory is a glazed room attached to a building. In practice couples use the word for any grand glazed reception room, so this directory covers conservatories, palm houses, orangeries, atriums, working greenhouses and the glasshouses inside botanical gardens. Filter by type if the distinction matters to you.

Which botanical garden wedding venues allow outside catering?

Public gardens commonly work from an approved-caterer list rather than a fully open one, and the list is usually published on their events page. It is the single biggest variable in a wedding budget, so confirm it before you fall for a room. Browse botanical garden venues.

Where can I find small greenhouse wedding venues under 50 guests?

Set the capacity filter to “Under 50 — micro-wedding”. It only matches listings whose capacity we have confirmed against the venue’s own page, so it will be empty until those are filled in — in the meantime, filter by type for greenhouses and conservatories, which tend to have the smaller rooms, and ask each venue what it releases for small weddings.

How much do greenhouse wedding venues cost?

There is no single answer worth quoting: the same size of room varies by a factor of ten between a municipal greenhouse and a private estate conservatory, and the catering model usually matters more than the site fee. Our cost guide sets out how to build an estimate from the venue’s own numbers.

Are greenhouse wedding venues rain-proof?

A fully enclosed one is — that is the practical case for the whole category. A venue that puts the ceremony outdoors and the reception under glass gives you a same-day fallback rather than full weather independence. See rain-proof outdoor wedding venues.

Can a greenhouse wedding work in winter?

In a heated room, yes, and it is often the cheapest time to book. Orangeries and modern double-glazed rooms hold heat well; single-glazed Victorian glasshouses are much harder to warm. Ask whether heating is included in the hire fee or billed separately, and what temperature the venue guarantees.

Are there greenhouse wedding venues in Italy?

Yes — historic limonaie and estate glasshouses host weddings across the country. See the Italy destination collection for listings and the legal requirements for US couples.
