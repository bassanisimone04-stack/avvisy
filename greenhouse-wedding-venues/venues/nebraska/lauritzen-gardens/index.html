What to ask before you enquire

Glasshouses differ from ordinary venues in four specific ways, and these are the questions that separate a room that works in your month from one that only photographs well:

  1. Seated capacity with a dance floor, and with the planting where it will be on the day. Glazed rooms lose floor area to beds and structural piers, so the headline number and the usable one often differ.
  2. Ventilation and what it holds. Opening roof vents, side louvers, shade cloth or mechanical cooling — and the temperature the venue will stand behind on a hot afternoon.
  3. Heating, and who pays for it. Included in the hire fee or billed separately, and what temperature is guaranteed in the coldest month you would consider.
  4. Power and floors. The electrical supply available to your caterer and band, and whether floor protection is required before anyone dances.

Our cost guide covers the line items those answers feed into.

Questions about this venue

How much does a wedding at Lauritzen Gardens cost?

We do not publish a price for Lauritzen Gardens because we have not confirmed one against the venue’s own material, and a wrong figure is worse than none. Request the current rate sheet from the venue directly, and use our greenhouse wedding cost guide to check the quote covers every line item.

How many guests can Lauritzen Gardens hold?

Capacity is published on the listing once we have confirmed it at the source; until then it shows as a dash rather than a guess. Ask the venue for the seated capacity with a dance floor and with the planting in place — glasshouse rooms lose more floor area to beds and structural piers than a plain hall does, so the headline number and the usable one often differ.

Is the venue rain-proof?

Ask whether the ceremony, the dinner and the dancing can all happen under a solid roof without moving anything on the day. A fully enclosed glasshouse means there is no weather call to make; a garden ceremony with a glazed reception room means there is a fallback but still a decision. See rain-proof outdoor wedding venues.

What should we ask about heat and ventilation?

Three things: how the room is ventilated (opening roof vents, side louvers, mechanical cooling), what temperature it holds on a hot afternoon, and whether winter heating is included in the hire fee or billed on top. These questions separate glasshouses that work year-round from those that only work in spring and autumn.
