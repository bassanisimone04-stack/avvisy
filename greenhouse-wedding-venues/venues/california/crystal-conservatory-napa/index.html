About The Crystal Conservatory

The Crystal Conservatory is a restored 1890s Foster & Pearson glasshouse standing on a working vineyard on Vine Hill Road, eight minutes north of downtown Napa. It was a commercial propagation house until 1974, derelict until 2017, and reopened in 2019 after a restoration that replaced 4,100 panes and added a motorised retractable roof across 40% of the span.

What makes it work as a wedding venue is not the glass, which plenty of venues have. It is the proportion: 6,400 square feet under a ridge that runs 26 feet at its peak, so a 180-guest dinner reads as generous rather than crowded, and the mature palms and tree ferns along the north bed give photographers a middle ground that most glasshouses lack. In flat winter light the room still reads warm, which is the single hardest thing to find in this category.

The ceremony choice is genuinely open: 180 seated inside among the beds, or 250 in the walled cutting garden immediately west, with a same-day switch and no re-set fee. That is the argument for a glasshouse over a marquee, and it is the reason this venue ranks first on our California greenhouse wedding venues list.

Light, heat and the seasons that work

Napa's glasshouse season is longer than most of California's. From late April to early June the room holds within a few degrees of ambient with the vents open, and the light through the west glazing at 6:30pm is the reason couples book eighteen months out. Late September and October are the other peak, coinciding with crush — expect vineyard traffic and premium pricing.

July and August afternoons are hot: not unusable, because the roof opens, but the venue itself steers summer couples to a 5:30pm start. Winter is the underrated date. Underfloor hydronic heating holds a guaranteed 68°F from October to April at no surcharge, rain on the glass is an asset rather than a crisis, and the site fee drops 38%. If that appeals, read our guide to rain-proof outdoor wedding venues.

Catering, bar and vendors

Catering is in-house with a $185 per head food-and-beverage minimum, charged on top of the $18,000 Saturday site fee — so a 150-guest Saturday starts at roughly $45,750 before flowers, music or rentals. Corkage is waived on Napa County wine, which is unusual and worth building the bar around. Outside caterers are not permitted. Photographers, florists and bands are fully open, with no preferred-vendor surcharge.

Accessibility and logistics

The site is step-free throughout, including the garden ceremony lawn, though the parking apron is firm-packed gravel rather than paved. Two accessible restrooms sit inside the adjoining barn. Eighty cars park on site; above that the venue stages a shuttle from downtown Napa. Music must end at 10:30pm and the site clear by midnight under the Napa County noise ordinance — a hard limit, enforced, and worth designing the timeline around from the start.

Questions about this venue

Can you get married inside the glasshouse if it rains?

Yes. The ceremony can move from the walled garden into the glasshouse on the day at no extra charge, and the room is set for both from the morning. This is the practical reason couples choose a greenhouse wedding venue over a tented garden — see rain-proof outdoor wedding venues.

How much does this greenhouse wedding venue cost?

The 2026 site fee is $18,000 on a Saturday, $12,500 Friday or Sunday and $7,400 on a weekday, May to October; winter rates are 38% lower. Catering is in-house with a $185 per head minimum on top, so a 150-guest Saturday starts at roughly $45,750 before flowers, music or rentals. Compare it against the state median in our greenhouse wedding cost guide.

How hot does The Crystal Conservatory get in August?

Twelve roof vents, eight side louvers and 60% shade cloth hold the room within about 4°F of outside temperature up to 95°F ambient. Above that, the retractable roof opens. Afternoon ceremonies in July and August are still discouraged; most summer couples start at 5:30pm.

Is outside catering allowed?

No. Catering is in-house with a $185 per head food-and-beverage minimum, on top of the site fee. Wine is corkage-free from Napa County producers, which is unusual and worth building the bar around. If open catering matters more to you than exclusivity, look at the botanical garden wedding venues instead.

What is the smallest wedding the venue will host?

There is no guest minimum, but the site fee does not scale down, so the room is rarely booked under 80 guests. For a smaller day under glass, see our small greenhouse wedding venues in California.

What other conservatory wedding venues are nearby?

Glasshouse at Filoli Ridge in Woodside is the closest room of comparable scale, and the Palm House at Arroyo Gardens in Pasadena is the large-capacity botanical garden alternative at roughly half the site fee. All of them are on our greenhouse wedding venues in California page.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes — the site is step-free throughout, including the garden ceremony lawn, with two accessible restrooms in the adjoining barn. The parking apron is firm-packed gravel rather than paved, so flag wheelchair users when you enquire and the team will stage drop-off at the glasshouse door.
