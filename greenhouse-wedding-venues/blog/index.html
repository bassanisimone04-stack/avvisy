Greenhouse wedding questions

Short answers. Each links to the guide that covers it properly.

How much does a greenhouse wedding cost?

It depends on the catering model, the day of the week and whether the venue is municipal or private far more than on the building itself. Our cost guide sets out every line item to ask about so you can build a real estimate from the venue’s own numbers.

What are rain-proof outdoor wedding venues?

Venues that give you the garden without the weather risk: a glazed room where the ceremony, dinner and dancing all happen under a solid roof. Read the guide.

Are small greenhouse wedding venues good for small weddings?

They suit them well — a room that would feel cavernous at 150 guests can be right at 40, and smaller rooms inside larger glasshouses are often released on weekdays. Our small greenhouse wedding venues guide covers what to ask and where to look.

What should I ask before booking conservatory wedding venues?

Five things, in writing: seated capacity with a dance floor and the planting in place; the ventilation and heating spec with a temperature the venue will stand behind; the catering policy and any minimum spend; the electrical supply available to your caterer and band; and whether floor protection is required for dancing.

Do botanical garden wedding venues have different rules?

Usually yes — approved-caterer lists, fixed load-in windows and public opening hours are common. They are often the largest glazed rooms available in a city, in exchange for less control over the day. Browse them here.
