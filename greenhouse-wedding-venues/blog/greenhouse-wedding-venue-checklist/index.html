The greenhouse wedding venue checklist: 20 questions to ask before you book

Every question a glasshouse venue raises that a generic wedding checklist does not, collected in one place — with an interactive version you can save.

Why a checklist, not a gut feeling

Glasshouse venues raise questions a generic wedding checklist does not: ventilation, heating, glazing, catering policy, load-in access. A single tour rarely surfaces all of them on its own, and the answers from one venue are easy to conflate with another by the time you have seen three or four. A written checklist, used the same way at every venue, fixes both problems.

The core question everything else supports

Can the ceremony, dinner and dancing all happen under solid cover without moving anything on the day? Every other item below is really a way of pressure-testing that one answer — see our rain-proof venues guide for the full reasoning.

The 20 questions

  1. Can the ceremony, dinner and dancing all happen under solid cover?
  2. What ventilation does the room have, and what does it hold on a hot afternoon?
  3. Is heating included in the hire fee, and what is the guaranteed minimum temperature?
  4. Is the glazing single or double, and how old is the structure?
  5. What is the seated capacity with a dance floor, with planting in place?
  6. Is catering in-house, an approved list, or fully open?
  7. If there is a minimum spend, what does it include?
  8. What is the alcohol policy?
  9. What is the load-in and load-out window, and the actual route?
  10. What is the electrical supply available to caterers and bands?
  11. What can and cannot be attached to the glass or ironwork?
  12. Is there an amplified-sound curfew?
  13. Will other visitors be present during a public-garden rental?
  14. What exact area does the rental cover — is it full exclusivity?
  15. Is there a minimum guest count as well as a maximum?
  16. Is floor protection required for dancing?
  17. What is the date-change and cancellation policy, in writing?
  18. Is there a rain or weather contingency for any outdoor portion?
  19. How far in advance does this venue typically book out for your date?
  20. What happens if a wedding runs over its booked time?

Using the checklist

Check items off as you get answers, or copy the list to send by email ahead of a tour so the venue can prepare written responses. Your progress on this page is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it per venue.

Quick answers

Venue checklist FAQs

What is the single most important question to ask a glasshouse venue?

Whether the ceremony, dinner and dancing can all happen under solid cover without moving anything on the day — it is the one question that separates a genuinely rain-proof venue from one that only partly is. Every other question on a checklist supports that one.

Should I bring a written checklist to a venue tour?

Yes, and ask for written answers where possible rather than verbal reassurance during the tour. Verbal answers are easy to forget or misremember by the time you compare three venues; a written answer becomes something you can hold the venue to later.

How many venues should I compare before booking?

Enough to have a real basis for comparison — three is a common minimum, since a single tour gives you no sense of what is normal versus unusual for the category. Use the same checklist at each one so the answers are actually comparable.
