The **Sardinia** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* touches both the north and
the south of the island. These are the three restaurants we can point to with an
address, according to CNN's guide to the second season.

## Mabrouk, in Alghero

In Alghero's old town, **[Mabrouk](/en/locale/mabrouk-alghero)** (Via Santa Barbara 4) is a
seafood restaurant. In the episode Tucci meets **Antonietta Salaris**, who prepares
**lobster alla catalana**, the dish that recalls Alghero's Catalan past.

## Luigi Pomata, in Cagliari

In Cagliari, at **Viale Regina Margherita 18**, **[Luigi Pomata](/en/locale/luigi-pomata-cagliari)**
is known as the king of tuna. He cooks in the tradition of **Carloforte**, the island of San
Pietro with ancient Ligurian roots. For Tucci he prepares bluefin tuna with pesto in a
pasta dish, *cassulli alla carlofortina*. The tuna is house-produced and can also be
bought in tins.

## Fradis Minoris, on the Nora lagoon

On the Nora lagoon, in **Pula**, **[Fradis Minoris](/en/locale/fradis-minoris-pula)** is chef
**Francesco Stara**'s restaurant, in the kitchen since 2020. It has a Michelin star and the
Green Star for sustainability: the cooking is no-waste, using the skin and bones of the
fish too. In the episode Stara prepares fregola with seafood.

## What we haven't listed

CNN also mentions a small bakery in Alghero and **Arimani**, a cooking school in a village
in the north of the island where chef Simonetta Bazzu preserves traditional recipes. We
haven't listed them because we have no verified addresses.
