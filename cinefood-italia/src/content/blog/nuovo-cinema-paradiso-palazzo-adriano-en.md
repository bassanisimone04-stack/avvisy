**Cinema Paradiso** (*Nuovo Cinema Paradiso*), the film by **Giuseppe Tornatore** that won the
Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film on 26 March 1990, chose **Palazzo Adriano**, a village in the
Sicani Mountains south of Palermo, as its main set. If you search **Cinema Paradiso: where was
it filmed**, the answer is its square, **Piazza Umberto I**. Here is what to see, the museum
(opened in 2023), how to get there and where to eat.

## The locations at a glance

- **Palazzo Adriano**: Piazza Umberto I, the film's square.
- **Other Sicilian places**, according to guides (list to be reconfirmed): Bagheria, Cefalù, Castelbuono, Lascari, Chiusa Sclafani, Santa Flavia, San
  Nicola l'Arena, Termini Imerese and the ruins of Poggioreale.

## Palazzo Adriano's square

**Piazza Umberto I** is the film's square, recognisable and almost unchanged since 1988. The
film's cinema **no longer exists**: its facade was built for the shoot. Guides name the churches
**Santa Maria Assunta** and **Santa Maria del Lume** (with its bell tower) among the scene's
backdrops.

## The Nuovo Cinema Paradiso museum

The **Museo Nuovo Cinema Paradiso** is in the **former town hall**, in Piazza Umberto I, whose
facade inspired the set. It opened on **4 August 2023**, at the start of the fourth Paradiso
Film Fest. The ground floor has production stills from Cristaldi Film, props and a model of the
historic centre; the first floor a multimedia area and a recreation of **Alfredo's projection booth**.

- **Address:** Piazza Umberto I, 90030 Palazzo Adriano ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo+Nuovo+Cinema+Paradiso+Palazzo+Adriano)).
- **Hours and ticket:** Museionline says **free** entry and open every day 9–13 and 15–19, but
  advises checking. Phone +39 091 8349933 (to be verified).

## Where to eat in Palazzo Adriano

**[Albergo Ristorante Pizzeria del Viale](/en/locale/bar-paradiso-palazzo-adriano)**, at **2 Via
XX Settembre** ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Albergo+Ristorante+Pizzeria+del+Viale+Palazzo+Adriano)): not among the
filming locations, but a real address in the film's village. Otherwise the cooking of the Sicani
Mountains is rustic: home-style bread, local cheeses, cured meats, almond sweets. (to be verified: hours and booking).

## How to get there from Palermo

Palazzo Adriano is about 80 km from Palermo, a little over 1 hour 30 by car (SS121 and SS189,
then the SS188). Without a car, the most practical way is train plus taxi for the last stretch.
