In the **Sicily** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy*, CNN follows
the actor across Bagheria, Palermo, Catania and Lampedusa. Here are the venues we can
point to, based on CNN's guide to the episode and trade magazines.

## Bagheria: I Pupi

In Bagheria, near Palermo, Tucci visits the Michelin-starred restaurant
**[I Pupi](/en/locale/i-pupi-bagheria)** by chef **Tony Lo Coco** and tastes
spaghetti with bottarga and a fish antipasto. An important note: the episode
shows the previous site, on Via del Cavaliere. In 2025 the restaurant moved into
the lower buildings of Villa Palagonia, the "villa of monsters", and the old site
is now the osteria TuMa.

## Catania: Mè Cumpari Turiddu

In Catania Tucci dines at **[Mè Cumpari Turiddu](/en/locale/me-cumpari-turiddu-catania)**,
owner **Roberta Capizzi**'s restaurant, at Piazza Turi Ferro 36, listed with the
Michelin Guide's Bib Gourmand. On the plate: donkey carpaccio and pasta alla
Norma.

## Palermo and Lampedusa

In Palermo the episode enters the 16th-century palace of Princess Stefania de
Raffadali, where he tastes a timballo; on Lampedusa he cooks with a sardine
fisherman and his wife: sardines in salsa verde and sarde a beccafico. These are
not venues open to the public, so we don't list them on the site.

## What to eat in Sicily, like Tucci

The episode brings out the island's signature dishes: **pasta alla Norma**,
spaghetti with **bottarga**, **timballo** and **sarde a beccafico**. For more
Sicilian film places read *[Sicily on Screen, at the
Table](/en/blog/sicilia-del-cinema-a-tavola)*, or the full guide to *[Stanley
Tucci in Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*.
