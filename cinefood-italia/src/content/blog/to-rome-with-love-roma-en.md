With *To Rome with Love* (2012), **Woody Allen** wrote his love letter to
**Rome**, after Paris and Barcelona. The film is split into **four separate
stories** set in the capital, with a cast boasting three Oscar winners —
Allen himself, **Roberto Benigni** and **Penélope Cruz** — plus five
nominees, including **Alec Baldwin** and **Jesse Eisenberg**. It also
marked Benigni's return to a major American film after *Pinocchio*
(2002). Here is an itinerary through the film's Rome and where to stop for a bite.

## The Rome of the film

*To Rome with Love* stages a postcard Rome, with places in the historic centre such as **Piazza del Campidoglio**
and the **Trevi Fountain**. It is not a single-location
film but a mosaic of the city — perfect for a stroll through the capital's most
beautiful spots.

## Where to eat in the Rome of the film

None of the venues on our list is among the film's locations. For a break near
**Piazza Navona**, a coffee or an aperitivo at the
**[Bar Navona](/en/locale/caffe-di-piazza-navona)** is a possible stop (it is not among the filming locations),
between Bernini's fountains and the atmosphere of the historic centre.

Rome is the city with the most food locations on our site: from historic
trattorias to elegant cafés. Discover them all on the **[Rome](/en/roma)** page.

## What to taste

The Rome of *To Rome with Love* is one of outdoor tables: treat yourself to a
gelato in the square, a **supplì**, and in the evening authentic Roman cuisine —
**cacio e pepe**, **carbonara**, **artichokes**. The best way to live the capital
like in the film.

You will find the connected stops on the
**[To Rome with Love](/en/film/to-rome-with-love)** page.

## In short

*To Rome with Love* is an invitation to lose yourself among the capital's squares.
Start from **[Bar Navona](/en/locale/caffe-di-piazza-navona)** (not a film location) and let Woody
Allen's sunny, ironic Rome guide you.
