Season two of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* has an episode on the **Veneto**
that runs from cicchetti to the lagoon islands. Here are the four Venetian venues from
the episode that we can point to, according to CNN's guide to the season.

## All'Arco, the San Polo bacaro

**[All'Arco](/en/locale/all-arco-venezia)**, at **San Polo 436**, a few steps from the
Rialto market, is a family-run bacaro specialising in **cicchetti**: baccalà
mantecato, anchovies, porchetta. Open since 1980 according to sources, it is a tiny
place, best eaten standing.

## Villa 600 on Torcello

On the island of **Torcello**, **[Villa 600](/en/locale/villa-600-torcello)** (Fondamenta
dei Borgognoni 12) is where Tucci finds out how Venetians cook duck: he orders the
classic duck ragù.

## Venissa on Mazzorbo

On **Mazzorbo**, **[Venissa](/en/locale/venissa-mazzorbo)** is a one-Michelin-star
restaurant tied to a winery, at Fondamenta Santa Caterina 3. Sources say it is open from
May to October: check its website.

## Orient Experience in Cannaregio

**[Orient Experience](/en/locale/orient-experience-venezia)**, at Rio Terà Farsetti
1847/B, is a restaurant staffed by former refugees with Afghan and Middle Eastern cooking.
In the episode Tucci presents it as a sign of the openness of Venice, a trading city.

## What to eat in Venice, like Tucci

**Cicchetti**, **duck ragù**, lagoon fish. For television's Venice also read *[Somebody
Feed Phil in Venice](/en/blog/somebody-feed-phil-venezia-ristoranti)*, *[Mr. Ripley at
Caffè Florian](/en/blog/mr-ripley-venezia-caffe-florian)* and the guide *[Stanley Tucci in
Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*.
