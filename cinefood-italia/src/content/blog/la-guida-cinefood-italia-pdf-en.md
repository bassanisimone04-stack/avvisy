After months spent finding, verifying and writing about the Italian
venues seen in films and TV shows, we've packed it all into one place:
**The CineFood Italia Guide**, a downloadable PDF built for anyone who
wants to take the directory along on a trip.

## What's inside

The **75 real venues** from the directory — the exact same level of
verification you'll find on the site, no invented examples — organised
by **18 regions**, with address, price range, the film or show it's
linked to, and the exact scene shot or set there. Plus **4 ready-made
itineraries**: a day in Rome between Via Veneto and Trastevere; a route
through Naples and the coast, from Forcella to Castellabate; a Sicily
tour on the trail of The Godfather, Montalbano and Il Postino; a Grand
Tour of the North through Turin, Milan, Crema, Venice and Trieste.

## Why a PDF, if the site is free

The site stays free and always up to date — we'll keep adding venues and
articles. The guide is built for anyone actually planning a trip: check
it offline, on a plane, without hunting page by page. And buying it gets
you both the English and Italian versions in the same package.

## Where to find it

The guide is available on the **[PDF Guide](/en/guida)** page, also
reachable from the top menu. Happy reading, and happy travels on the
trail of the big screen.
