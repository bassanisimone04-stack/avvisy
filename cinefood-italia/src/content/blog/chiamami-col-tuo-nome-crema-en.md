Luca Guadagnino's Oscar-winning *Call Me by Your Name* turned **Crema** and its
surroundings into a film-tourism destination. The town even offers an
**official itinerary** — on foot or by bike — through the film's locations. Here
are the main stops and where to treat yourself to a tasty break.

## The locations in central Crema

The heart of the itinerary is the scenic **[Piazza del Duomo](/en/crema)**, where
the scene of Elio and Oliver sitting at a café table is set. A short walk away,
under the **Torrazzo Arch**, is the meeting between Elio and Marzia. Strolling
through the arcades and squares of the centre feels like leafing through the
film's frames.

For a coffee or an aperitivo with a view of the Duomo, the natural stop is the
**[Caffè del Duomo](/en/locale/caffe-del-duomo-crema)**, overlooking the very
square that symbolises the film.

## What to taste in Crema

Crema's cuisine is a little surprise: try the **tortelli cremaschi**, with a
sweet-spiced filling of amaretti, raisins and mostaccino — a truly unique dish.
In summer, the ritual is gelato or granita in the shade of the arcades.

## The itinerary through the natural locations

Outside the town, the film turns bucolic: the **Laghetto dei Riflessi** in
Ricengo is where Elio and Oliver swim, surrounded by the Crema countryside. An
ideal itinerary for a slow weekend of cinema, art and food.

You will find all the connected stops on the
**[Call Me by Your Name](/en/film/chiamami-col-tuo-nome)** page and the venues of
**[Crema](/en/crema)**.

## In short

Crema proves that film tourism can also be intimate and refined: a square, a café,
a plate of tortelli. Start from the
**[Caffè del Duomo](/en/locale/caffe-del-duomo-crema)** and let the film's mood
guide you.
