In the **Umbria** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy*, the first person the
actor meets is **Giorgione**, a TV chef and cookbook author. He meets him at his
restaurant in **Montefalco**, according to CNN's guide to the second season.

## Alla Via di Mezzo, in Montefalco

**[Alla Via di Mezzo da Giorgione](/en/locale/alla-via-di-mezzo-montefalco)** is at **Via
di Santa Chiara da Montefalco 52**, which the restaurant's website also gives as Via
Verdi 52, the street's old name: use the latter for GPS. The restaurant is housed in a
**14th-century oil mill**, a short walk from the centre of the village. Giorgione is
Giorgio Barchesi, known as Big George.

## What we haven't listed

CNN also mentions **Norcineria Fratelli Ansuini** in Norcia, devoted to pork, and **La
Prosciutteria** in Perugia, a deli with cured meats and cheeses. These are shops rather
than restaurants with an address to book, so they stay out.
