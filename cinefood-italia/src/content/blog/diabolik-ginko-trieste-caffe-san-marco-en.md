To recreate **Clerville**, the fictional city from the Diabolik comic,
the **Manetti Bros.** borrowed the architecture of three Italian
cities: Bologna, Milan and **Trieste**. And it's Trieste that gives the
film one of its most spectacular moments.

## The chase in Piazza della Borsa

In one of the film's most talked-about scenes, Inspector Ginko's
(**Valerio Mastandrea**) police cars chase **Diabolik**'s (**Giacomo
Gianniotti**) legendary Jaguar E-Type right through **Piazza della
Borsa**, one of Trieste's most scenic squares — with vintage cars and
period-costumed extras recreating the comic's retro-futuristic mood.

## The scene at Caffè San Marco

A short walk away, the Manetti Bros. chose the **[Antico Caffè San
Marco](/en/locale/caffe-san-marco-trieste)** for a scene at the venue's
counter — the same literary café, open since 1914, that also appeared
in the Sky series *[Il Re](/en/blog/il-re-trieste-caffe-san-marco)*
starring Luca Zingaretti. A double credit for one of Italy's most
photographed historic cafés.

## Trieste, a recurring set

With its Mitteleuropean architecture — arcades, Liberty-style palazzi,
squares facing the sea — Trieste keeps attracting productions looking
for a timeless aesthetic, one that can transform into a fictional city
without losing its own identity.
