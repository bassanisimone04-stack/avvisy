In the **Liguria** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* the actor goes from the
showcase of Portofino to Genoa's old town, where he learns that pesto is made by hand. These
are the two venues from the episode that we can point to, according to CNN's guide to the
second season.

## Cracco Portofino, on the quay

**[Cracco Portofino](/en/locale/cracco-portofino)** is at **Molo Umberto I 9**, right on the water.
It has a Michelin star (2026 guide) and has been open since **2021** in the historic
premises of the former Pitosforo. In the episode Tucci meets **Carlo Cracco**, and executive
chef **Mattia Pecis** prepares **pansotti preboggion**: herb-filled pasta with a walnut
sauce. The menu is tasting-only, 7 or 11 courses.

## Il Genovese, in Genoa

At **Via Galata 35**, in central Genoa, **[Il Genovese](/en/locale/il-genovese-genova)** is run by
**Roberto Panizza** with his brother Sergio. Here the pesto is crushed by hand in an
ancient mortar, with Genoese DOP basil, pine nuts, Vessalico garlic and Ligurian oil. The
signature dish is **gnocchi with pesto**, next to trofie and pansotti.

## What we haven't listed

CNN also mentions **The Cook**, Ivano Ricchebono's haute cuisine in a frescoed Genoa
palazzo, where Tucci tastes cappon magro. The restaurant has moved and we have no
verified current address, so for now we don't publish it.
