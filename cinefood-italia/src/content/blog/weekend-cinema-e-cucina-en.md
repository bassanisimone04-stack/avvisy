A "cinematic" weekend is the perfect way to combine travel, cinema and food.
Here is how to plan it in a few steps, with some ready-made ideas to start from.

## 1. Choose the film (or the city)

Start from a title you love or a city you want to visit. In our [Films](/en/film)
section you'll find the works with their linked venues; in the city pages (like
[Rome](/en/roma)) you'll find all the sets of a single destination. For a
first trip, compact villages like **[Savoca](/en/savoca)** or
**[Crema](/en/crema)** are easier to explore on foot than a big city.

## 2. Build the itinerary

Alternate **locations** and **food stops**. Use the [map](/en/mappa) to see
which venues are close to each other and avoid too much travelling. A good
pace is two or three stops a day: better to enjoy a few places well than
cram in too many.

## 3. Book in advance

The most famous restaurants fill up fast, especially in high season
(summer, long weekends, holidays). Check the "booking" field on the venue
pages and always confirm opening hours and closing days — many historic
venues close one day a week.

## 4. Tell real sets from themed venues

Not all venues are real sets: some are [cinema-themed](/en/locali). Both
are fun, but it's nice to know what to expect —
[here's the difference explained](/en/blog/set-reale-o-a-tema) in detail.

## 5. Leave room for the unexpected

Film villages are small and surprising: treat yourself to an extra stroll,
a coffee in the square, an improvised granita. Often it's the unplanned
stop that becomes the best memory of the trip.

Ready to go? Browse the [venues](/en/locali) and create your itinerary.
