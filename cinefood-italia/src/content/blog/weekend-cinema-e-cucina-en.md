A "cinematic" weekend is the perfect way to combine travel, cinema and food.
Here is how to plan it in a few steps.

## 1. Choose the film (or the city)

Start from a title you love or a city you want to visit. In our [Films](/en/film)
section you'll find the works with their linked venues; in the city pages (like
[Rome](/en/roma)) you'll find all the sets of a single destination.

## 2. Build the itinerary

Alternate **locations** and **food stops**. Use the [map](/en/mappa) to see which
venues are close to each other and avoid too much travelling.

## 3. Book in advance

The most famous restaurants fill up fast. Check the "booking" field on the venue
pages and always confirm opening hours and closing days.

## 4. Tell real sets from themed venues

Not all venues are real sets: some are [cinema-themed](/en/locali). Both are fun,
but it's nice to know what to expect.

## 5. Leave room for the unexpected

Film villages are small and surprising: treat yourself to an extra stroll, a
coffee in the square, an improvised granita.

Ready to go? Browse the [venues](/en/locali) and create your itinerary.
