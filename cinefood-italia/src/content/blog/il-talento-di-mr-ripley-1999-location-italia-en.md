**The Talented Mr. Ripley**, Anthony Minghella's 1999 film with Matt Damon and Jude Law, was
shot almost entirely in Italy, but not where many think: the **Atrani** coast belongs to the
2024 Netflix series, while the film uses the islands of **Procida** and **Ischia** for the
fictional town of **Mongibello**, then **Rome** and **Venice**. If you search *The Talented Mr.
Ripley filming locations*, here are the locations sources confirm and those still to verify.

## The locations at a glance

- **Procida** (Marina Corricella, Via San Rocco): Mongibello.
- **Ischia** (Ischia Ponte, waterfront, Piazzale Aragonese): Mongibello.
- **Rome**: Piazza Navona, Piazza di Spagna, Capitoline Hill and the Imperial Forums, Piazza Mattei.
- **Venice**: Piazza San Marco and **Caffè Florian**.
- **Naples**: the Teatro San Carlo, according to Italy for Movies.

## Procida and Ischia: the film's Mongibello

Mongibello doesn't exist: it is the fictional town where Tom meets Dickie and Marge, played by
two islands in the Bay of Naples. In **Procida** you see **Marina Corricella**, the fishing
village with colourful houses, and Via San Rocco (the same houses as *Il Postino*: read *[Il
Postino in Procida](/en/blog/il-postino-procida)*). In **Ischia** location catalogues cite
**Ischia Ponte**, the Piazza Malcovati waterfront and the Piazzale Aragonese, in front of the
Aragonese Castle. You get there from Naples or Pozzuoli by ferry or hydrofoil. The exact match

## Rome in the film

In Rome location lists name **Piazza Navona**, **Piazza di Spagna**, the **Capitoline Hill** with
its view over the Imperial Forums and **Piazza Mattei**. A single list also cites **Caffè
Latino** in Testaccio (to be verified). To eat in the Rome of the movies read *[Rome in the movies: 5
film restaurants](/en/blog/roma-nel-cinema-ristoranti-da-film)*.

## Venice and Caffè Florian

The ending is in Venice, on **Piazza San Marco**. At **[Caffè Florian](/en/locale/caffe-florian)**,
under the Procuratie Nuove, Tom meets Marge and Dickie's father. Open since 1720, it is
considered Italy's oldest café still in operation, with frescoed rooms. Address: **57 Piazza San
Marco, 30124 Venice** ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caffe+Florian+Venezia)). Only one site also cites the **Westin Europa
& Regina** hotel (to be verified). The guide to the scene is in *[The Talented Mr. Ripley in Venice:
Caffè Florian](/en/blog/mr-ripley-venezia-caffe-florian)*.

## 1999 film or 2024 Netflix series?

Same story, two productions and different locations. The film (1999) uses Procida, Ischia, Rome
and Venice. The series *Ripley* (2024) was shot in **Atrani** and on the Amalfi Coast: the guide
is *[Ripley on Netflix: where it was filmed, Atrani and Amalfi](/en/blog/ripley-netflix-atrani-costiera-amalfitana)*.
For the film **I found no source** for Atrani, Positano or Sanremo, nor for Porto Ercole or
Cinecittà, so we don't include them.
