There's a detail that surprises many viewers of *Blocco 181 – Gangs of
Milano*: the Sky series about youth gangs in Milan's suburbs, created by
rapper **Salmo**, isn't filmed in Milan at all. Season 2 was shot almost
entirely in **Turin**.

## Turin becomes Milan

According to the **Film Commission Torino Piemonte**, filming for
season 2 (October 2023 - April 2024) involved **over a hundred**
Piedmont locations, transformed on screen into the gritty outer Milan
the series portrays. Among them: Le Roi Music Hall and the Crocetta
district.

## Del Cambio, from luxury to the suburbs

One of the locations involved is particularly striking:
**[Ristorante Del Cambio](/en/locale/del-cambio-torino)**, on Piazza
Carignano — one of Italy's most elegant and historic restaurants, open
since 1757, today holding a Michelin star. A sharp contrast with the
gritty aesthetic of the suburbs the series portrays, and proof of how
versatile Turin's locations can be across very different genres.

## Not its first time on set

Del Cambio is no stranger to filming: the same restaurant also hosted
production of *[Corro da te](/en/blog/corro-da-te-torino-del-cambio)*
(2022), the romantic comedy starring Pierfrancesco Favino and Miriam
Leone — further proof of how Turin, and its historic addresses, keeps
attracting productions of completely opposite genres.

## In short

*Blocco 181* confirms an unwritten rule of Italian cinema: Turin can
become any city, and its historic venues can adapt to entirely
different stories. Discover more **[films and shows](/en/film)** shot
in Italy, or the full list of **[movie restaurants](/en/blog/ristoranti-veri-nei-film-italia-lista)**.
