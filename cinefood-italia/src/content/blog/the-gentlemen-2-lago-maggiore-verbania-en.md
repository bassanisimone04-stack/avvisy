Season two of ***The Gentlemen***, the Netflix series created by **Guy Ritchie**, takes
Eddie Horniman to Italy. Many assumed Lake Como: the Italian set is in fact **Lake
Maggiore**, on the Piedmont shore, in **Verbania**.

## The locations: Verbania and Pallanza

According to the list of sets from **Film Commission Torino Piemonte**, picked up by the
local press, filming centred on the **Pallanza** district of Verbania. The places named are:

- **Villa Rusconi-Clerici**, a neoclassical lakeside villa with tiered Italian gardens:
  a temporary pier was built for the shoot, and characters arrive there by speedboat;
- **Piazza Garibaldi** and **Via Vittorio Veneto**, for the outdoor scenes in central
  Pallanza;
- the **church of San Leonardo**, for the wedding ceremony at the heart of episode six;
- the **Grand Hotel Majestic**, a Belle Époque hotel used as the crew's base and
  appearing in the series.

Filming also reached **Intra** and **Stresa**, with the **Borromean Islands** in the
background. The Italian work took place between **June and July 2025**, concentrated into
about two weeks; the season landed on Netflix on **3 September 2026**.

## Where to eat: the Grand Hotel Majestic

The one address on the list where you can also sit down to eat is the
**[Grand Hotel Majestic](/en/locale/la-beola-grand-hotel-majestic-verbania)**, at Via
Vittorio Veneto 32, on a headland in Pallanza. Its restaurant is called **La Beola** and
serves lake fish, seafood and meat; in summer it gives way to the **Il Portico** terrace
overlooking the garden. The hotel is open to the public, so you don't need to be a guest
to book a table.

One detail worth stating plainly: the sources document **the hotel** as a location and as
the crew's base, not the restaurant room in particular.

## A set that is not Lake Como

It bears repeating, because it is the most common mistake of these months: the speedboats,
villas and period hotels of the season are **not on Lake Como**. If you want to retrace
the trip, head for Verbania and Lake Maggiore, just over an hour by train from Milan.
