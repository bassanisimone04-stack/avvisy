The **Piedmont** episode of season two of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* pairs
two very different addresses: a historic Turin restaurant and a provincial risotteria.

## Turin: Ristorante Del Cambio

CNN's guide lists among the episode's restaurants **[Del Cambio](/en/locale/del-cambio-torino)**,
Turin's oldest restaurant, open since 1757 at **Piazza Carignano 2**. It is the same venue
seen in *[Corro da te](/en/blog/corro-da-te-torino-del-cambio)* and in *[Blocco
181](/en/blog/blocco-181-torino-del-cambio)*.

## Vercelli: Christian e Manuel

In **Vercelli**, Italy's rice capital, Tucci goes to
**[Christian e Manuel](/en/locale/christian-e-manuel-vercelli)**, at **Corso Magenta 71**: a
one-Michelin-star risotteria run by two brothers at the back of Hotel Cinzia, with over twenty
risottos on the menu. CNN also devoted a separate article to Vercelli's risotto.

## Before you go

Del Cambio is closed on Mondays according to its listing, and Christian e Manuel is open Tuesday to Saturday for lunch and dinner, with Sunday hours to be confirmed. Both are real restaurants open to the public: check hours and book ahead, especially for the starred one.

## What to eat in Piedmont, like Tucci

**Risotto**, and Piedmontese cooking in general. For Turin on screen also read *[Corro da te and
Del Cambio](/en/blog/corro-da-te-torino-del-cambio)*, and for the other episodes *[Stanley Tucci in
Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*.
