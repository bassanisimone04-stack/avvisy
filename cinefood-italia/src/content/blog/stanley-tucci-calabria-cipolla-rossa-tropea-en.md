The **Calabria** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* is the most personal of the
series: it is the region his family comes from. His grandfather was from **Marzi**, in the
province of Cosenza, his grandmother from Serra San Bruno. Here is the restaurant from
the episode that we can point to, according to CNN's guide to the second season.

## Osteria della Cipolla Rossa, near Tropea

**[Osteria della Cipolla Rossa](/en/locale/osteria-della-cipolla-rossa-tropea)** (Via Lungomare,
Tropea) is a tiny place with a **vegetarian** menu that changes daily. The chef is
**Romana Schiariti**, and her husband Michele is the host. Its dish is spaghetti with
**Tropea red onion**, an onion so sweet that Tucci, in the episode, bites into it raw like
an apple. Sources say **booking is mandatory**.

## The bread of Marzi

In Tucci's grandfather's village CNN mentions **Panificio Cuti**, Pina Olivetti's bakery,
which has made *pane di cuti* since 1985 from a hundred-year-old recipe. For Tucci and
his parents she also makes *morsello*, a hollowed-out bread filled with sausage and
broccoli rabe. It isn't a restaurant and we haven't listed it.

## What we haven't listed

CNN also mentions **Il Principe di Scilla**, a family-run restaurant focused on
swordfish. The sources we found list it as temporarily closed, so for now we
don't publish it.
