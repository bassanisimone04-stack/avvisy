Among the many comedies shot in Florence, *I laureati* (1995) by **Leonardo
Pieraccioni** has a detail food lovers will enjoy: one of its locations is a
trattoria that still exists, **[Trattoria Coco
Lezzone](/en/locale/coco-lezzone-firenze)**. Italy for Movies, the Ministry of
Culture's location portal, lists a scene shot inside the venue.

## The trattoria

Trattoria Coco Lezzone stands on **Via del Parioncino 26/r**, in the historic
centre, a short walk from Ponte Santa Trinita. It is listed among Florence's
historic businesses and serves classic Tuscan cooking without frills.

## What we know (and what we don't)

The sources we could consult confirm that a scene of the film is shot inside
the trattoria. They don't say which one, or how much of the venue appears on
screen, so we don't tell details we can't verify. You'll find the film's page
at **[I laureati](/en/film/i-laureati)**.

## What to eat in Florence, as at Coco Lezzone

Florentine food is made of humble, hearty dishes: **pappa al pomodoro**,
**ribollita**, roast pork **arista** and, for the signature dish, **bistecca
alla fiorentina**. To drink, a Tuscan red.

## An itinerary in central Florence

After lunch the centre is all within walking distance: Ponte Vecchio, Piazza
della Signoria and **[Caffè Rivoire](/en/locale/rivoire-firenze)**, which
appears in *Hannibal*, or the trattoria from the series *From Scratch*,
**[Buca dell'Orafo](/en/locale/buca-dellorafo-firenze)**. See all the city's
venues on the **[Florence](/en/firenze)** page.
