*Under the Tuscan Sun* (2003) made millions of travellers dream with its golden
**Tuscany**. The protagonist Frances buys and restores a villa in **Cortona**, a
splendid town in the province of Arezzo, rediscovering herself among hills, wine
and genuine cooking.

## The Cortona of the film

Cortona is an Etruscan gem overlooking the Val di Chiana: steep alleys, medieval
squares and endless views over the vineyards. The film's villa, "Bramasole", has
become a small place of pilgrimage.

## The flavours of Tuscany

Tuscan cuisine is honest and memorable. Not to be missed:

- The **bistecca alla fiorentina**, thick and rare;
- **Pici**, the hand-rolled local pasta, with ragù or cacio e pepe;
- **Ribollita** and **pappa al pomodoro**;
- The great **red wines** (Chianti, Nobile di Montepulciano, Brunello) and the
  extra-virgin olive oil.

## An itinerary between hills and wineries

Cortona is the ideal base for a slow itinerary through villages (Montepulciano,
Pienza), wineries and farmhouses. The film's slow pace is truly in the air.

## In short

*Under the Tuscan Sun* is Tuscany as we dream it: sun, wine and rebirth. Cortona
is the perfect starting point for a tasty journey. Discover the other
**[films](/en/film)** and their food locations.
