There's a true love story behind *From Scratch*, the Netflix limited
series starring **Zoe Saldana**: the one between Tembi Locke, author of
the memoir the show is based on, and the Sicilian chef she met during a
study stay in Florence.

## Amy and Lino, between Florence and Sicily

The series follows Amy, an American student in Florence, who falls for
**Lino**, a Sicilian chef. The story moves between the historic centre of
Florence and Sicily, weaving the couple's everyday life together with the
more dramatic turns of their journey — faithful, in its essential
outline, to the real story Tembi Locke tells in her book.

## Buca dell'Orafo, the real venue

In the series, Lino works as a chef at
**[Buca dell'Orafo](/en/locale/buca-dellorafo-firenze)**, a historic
Florentine trattoria on Via dei Girolami, a short walk from **Ponte
Vecchio**. The name recalls the Florentine tradition of "buche" —
historic venues set at street level or below ground in the old city
centre — and Buca dell'Orafo is one of the centre's historic trattorias, serving
straightforward, no-frills Tuscan cooking.

## An itinerary along the show's locations

Anyone visiting Florence to follow the series can pair a stop at Buca
dell'Orafo with a walk across Ponte Vecchio and through the Uffizi, both
minutes away on foot. It's an itinerary that combines postcard Florence
with the more authentic city — neighbourhood trattorias open for
generations.
