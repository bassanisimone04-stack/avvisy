With *The Hand of God*, **Paolo Sorrentino** told the story of his own
Naples — his teenage years and a family tragedy that shaped his life —
in a largely autobiographical film, winner of the Silver Lion in Venice
and an Oscar nominee.

## Sorrentino's Vomero

The Schisa family home is filmed on **Via San Domenico**, in the
**Vomero** district, just one floor from the director's real childhood
home. Among the other real locations: the **Galleria Umberto I**, where
Fabietto meets director Antonio Capuano, **Piazza del Plebiscito**, the
Gulf of Naples, and the Maradona stadium.

## Villa Giusso, the family lunch

One of the film's most intense scenes — the big family lunch — is shot
at **Villa Giusso**, an agriturismo set in a 17th-century former
monastery in the hills above **Vico Equense**, on the Sorrentine
Peninsula, at about 400 metres altitude overlooking the Gulf of Naples.
It's a genuinely public venue, with a kitchen built around the estate's
own produce.

## In short

*The Hand of God* is an intimate, autobiographical portrait by
Sorrentino, shot between real Naples streets and the Sorrentine
