*My Brilliant Friend*, the HBO/Rai series based on Elena Ferrante's
novels, made 1950s Naples famous worldwide — but not everything you see
is filmed in the real city.

## The neighborhood is a set, not a real place

The heart of the story, the Rione Luzzatti where Lila and Elena grow up,
is actually a reconstruction on roughly 20,000 square metres at the
former Saint-Gobain glass factory in **Marcianise**, in the province of
Caserta, about 45 minutes from Naples. A common production choice for
period series, letting the team control every detail of 1950s Naples
without having to "age" modern streets.

## The real Naples locations

Many other scenes, though, are shot in the real city: the scenic
**Galleria Principe di Napoli**, opposite the National Archaeological
Museum, the **Caracciolo seafront**, the historic bookshops of
**Port'Alba**, **Piazza dei Martiri**, the **Teatro San Carlo** and **Via
Chiaia**. The seaside scenes are shot instead on **Ischia** and **Gaeta**.

## ScottoJonno, the real venue in the Gallery

Season 4 (2024) features **[ScottoJonno](/en/locale/scottojonno-napoli)**,
a café-bistrot inside the
Galleria Principe di Napoli, reopened in 2023 by entrepreneur Luca
Iannuzzi in a 19th-century former café-chantant, with cooking by chef
Marco Ambrosino. It's a genuinely public venue, not a set.

## In short

*My Brilliant Friend* mixes a built set with real Naples streets and
venues — a great excuse for a walk through the Galleria Principe, the
seafront and Port'Alba. If you're staying in the city, also discover the
Naples of **[The Hand of God](/en/blog/mano-di-dio-napoli-villa-giusso)**
and the pizza of **[Eat Pray Love](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)**,
or explore more **[films and shows](/en/film)** shot in Italy on our
site.
