**Where was The Devil Wears Prada 2 filmed?** Twenty years after the first film,
Miranda Priestly and Andy Sachs are back in Italy, and the locations are two:
**Milan**, the fashion capital, and **Lake Como**. The film is already out
(Italy 29 April 2026, United States 1 May), but the Milan shoot dates from
**October 2025**. Here are the places that reliable sources support, the ones
that remain rumours, and where you can really eat and stay.

## The locations at a glance

- **Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa**, Milan: the cast's base, with scenes in the
  lobby, at Caffè Parigi and outside (the hotel says so itself).
- **Brera**: a large street scene with hundreds of extras and, on 8 October 2025,
  a set at the **Pinacoteca di Brera** (LaPresse).
- **Fashion Quadrilateral and Fashion Week**: filming during the real shows.
- **Villa Arconati**, Castellazzo di Bollate: Corte Nobile, grand staircase and Sala
  di Fetonte (Falstaff).
- **Villa Balbiano**, Lake Como: Benji Barnes's villa (Elite Traveler).

## Palazzo Parigi and Caffè Parigi: the film's Milan base

The **Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa** is at **Corso di Porta Nuova 1**, between
Brera and the Quadrilateral. On its official website the hotel says it welcomed the
cast and crew of *The Devil Wears Prada 2*, with scenes shot in the **lobby**, at
**Caffè Parigi** and in the **exterior spaces**. Some outlets reported that the
lobby was screened with black drapes to hide the shoot.

One caveat: a single travel guide claims the **bedrooms** seen in the film were
rebuilt in the United States and are not the hotel's. I couldn't confirm it, so
don't book a suite hoping for the film's room.

For the food angle: **[Caffè Parigi](/en/locale/caffe-parigi-milano)** is the
hotel's café-bistro, open to the city and not just to guests. If you want the set
atmosphere without sleeping there, it's the right move: a coffee or an aperitivo,
then a walk around Brera.

## Brera, the Fashion Quadrilateral and the real Fashion Week

The film doesn't recreate Milan fashion: it films it while it happens. According
to the press, during **Fashion Week** Meryl Streep and Stanley Tucci were seen in
costume and in character in the front row at the **Dolce & Gabbana** show. In the
shooting days a street scene with hundreds of extras was staged in Brera, and on
8 October 2025 the production was at the **Pinacoteca di Brera**. Some guides
mention the Brera Academy instead: sources disagree, and here we stick to what
LaPresse reported.

Other guides list the **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II**, Piazza Duomo and Palazzo
Clerici among the locations. These are travel-blog lists: you can visit them anyway,
but I found no confirmation from the production. The same goes for the **Bulgari
Hotel Milano** and the **Teatro alla Scala**, which circulate in some lists but for
which I found no source: that's why we leave them out.

To eat in the same Milan of the movies, also read
*[House of Gucci in Milan](/en/blog/house-of-gucci-milano)* and the guide to
*[Stanley Tucci in Milan](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-milano-ratana-osteria-del-treno)*.

## Villa Arconati: the villa outside Milan

In **Castellazzo di Bollate**, a few kilometres from the centre, **Villa Arconati**
(called Lombardy's «little Versailles») was one of the film's Fashion Week sets.
According to Falstaff the scenes were shot in the **Corte Nobile**, on the **grand
staircase** and in the **Sala di Fetonte**. The villa is run by the Fondazione
Augusto Rancilio and is visited mostly on Sundays: a ticketing listing shows
openings every Sunday until 13 December 2026, from 11 am to 7 pm. Hours and prices
change every season: check
[villaarconati-far.it](https://www.villaarconati-far.it) before you leave.

## Villa Balbiano: the film's Lake Como

For the lake part, Elite Traveler names **Villa Balbiano**, in Ossuccio, as
**Benji Barnes's** villa. According to the same source, the production scouted the
whole lake to find it, and the lawn and sculpture garden were added for the film. It
is the same villa seen in *House of Gucci*: its history is in
*[House of Gucci in Milan and on the lake](/en/blog/house-of-gucci-milano)* and more
lake locations in *[Lake Como in the movies](/en/blog/lago-di-como-nel-cinema-dove-mangiare)*.

**Can you visit it?** Not as a tourist attraction. It is a private home, which
according to Elite Traveler can be rented exclusively, for up to 30 guests. If you
want a lake villa you can really visit, look for **Villa del Balbianello** in Lenno:
a different villa with a similar name.

## When and how it was shot

The Milan shoot took place between late September and October 2025, according to
the Italian press: the exact dates vary from source to source, so we don't state
them. Sky TG24 reported in May 2026 a rise in tourist interest in the film's
Italian locations.

## Plan your itinerary

1. **Morning in Brera**: the Pinacoteca and the neighbourhood's streets.
2. **Break at [Caffè Parigi](/en/locale/caffe-parigi-milano)**, a short walk away.
3. **Afternoon in the Quadrilateral**, among the fashion houses' windows.
4. **A separate day** for Villa Arconati (on a Sunday) or for Lake Como.

More films and series shot in Milan and around are on the
**[Milan](/en/milano)** page, or on the film's page for
**[The Devil Wears Prada 2](/en/film/the-devil-wears-prada-2)**.
