*My Brilliant Friend*, the series based on Elena Ferrante's novels, has turned
Naples and the places around Lila and Elena into a destination for film tourism
fans. But the four seasons were not all shot in one place: here is the map,
season by season. For a place to stop in Naples, see the café
**[ScottoJonno](/en/blog/amica-geniale-napoli-scottojonno)** in our other
article on the series.

## The rione Luzzatti: real in Naples, rebuilt near Caserta

The **rione Luzzatti** does exist: it is a working-class neighbourhood on the
eastern edge of Naples, in the Poggioreale area, next to the industrial zones
of Gianturco. For the first seasons, however, the production rebuilt the 1950s
neighbourhood on a lot of about 20,000 square metres at the former
**Saint-Gobain** factory outside Caserta. In season four, set in the 1980s,
the rione returns in brighter colours.

The city of **Caserta** also appears in several scenes: Corso Trieste, Piazza
Dante and the area in front of the Royal Palace are among the places named by
the trade press, together with Casertavecchia.

## Season by season

- **Season one**: Naples, with Piazza del Plebiscito, and the rione rebuilt
  near Caserta.
- **Season two**: filming moves to **Ischia**, notably **Forio**, for the
  protagonists' summer.
- **Season three**: besides Naples and Caserta, **Florence** arrives, with
  Piazza della Signoria and Piazza Santa Croce, and **Turin**, the setting of
  Elena's university life. In Naples filming also touched Piazza del Gesù and
  Piazza Dante.
- **Season four** (directing passes to Laura Bispuri): via Petrarca, the
  Caracciolo seafront and Piazza del Plebiscito again in Naples, then Turin with
  the support of Film Commission Torino Piemonte and a scene set in Tuscany.

## How to visit

- In **Naples** the easiest route starts in the centre: Piazza del Plebiscito,
  the Caracciolo seafront and Piazza Dante are close to each other. The rione
  Luzzatti, on the outskirts, is a residential neighbourhood and not a set:
  visit it respectfully.
- In **Caserta** the Saint-Gobain set was a production area: it is not a
  tourist destination.
- On **Ischia** and in **Florence** the locations are public places, open to
  everyone.
