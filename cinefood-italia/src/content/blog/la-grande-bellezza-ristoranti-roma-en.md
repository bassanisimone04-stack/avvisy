Paolo Sorrentino's *The Great Beauty*, winner of the Oscar for Best Foreign
Language Film in **2014**, is a long declaration of love to **Rome**, with
**Toni Servillo** as Jep Gambardella, a failed writer drifting through
parties and disillusionment in Rome's high society. Amid night-time
terraces, palaces and decadent parties, the film also offers unforgettable
scenes at the table. Here is where to find Jep's Rome, fork in hand.

## Where Jep Gambardella dines

One of the film's most elegant dinners is set at the
**[Veranda dell'Hotel Columbus](/en/locale/la-veranda-hotel-columbus)**, in Borgo
Santo Spirito, a stone's throw from St Peter's, inside Palazzo della Rovere.
This is where Jep dines with Ramona, in one of the most refined Roman sequences.

**Please note:** Hotel Columbus was closed after an eviction and the restaurant
no longer appears to be operating, so you can't dine there. We keep it in the
directory as a record of the set.

## Palazzo Sacchetti, Viola's home

Another key location in the film is **Palazzo Sacchetti**, on Via Giulia:
this is where Viola lives, one of the characters in Jep's circle of
friends, in Renaissance halls frescoed by **Francesco Salviati**. The
palace's front door becomes, in the film, the entrance to the residence of
the Colonna counts, while the interiors host several scenes with Viola's
son, Andrea.

## The cinematic Rome to experience

Beyond the table, *The Great Beauty* itinerary touches some of the city's most
beautiful places: the **Fontanone del Gianicolo**, the **Capitoline Museums**,
**Palazzo Spada**, **Villa Medici** and the Aventine gardens. A route that
combines art, panoramas and the night-time magic of the capital.

## What to taste in Rome

To complete the experience, treat yourself to authentic Roman cuisine: **cacio e
pepe**, **carbonara**, **Jewish-style artichokes**, **oxtail stew**. Rome is also
the city with the most food locations on our site: discover them all on the
**[Rome](/en/roma)** page.

You will find the connected stops on the
**[The Great Beauty](/en/film/la-grande-bellezza)** page.

## In short

Retracing *The Great Beauty* today means following Jep among the terraces,
fountains and palaces of Rome. The dinner at the
**[Veranda dell'Hotel Columbus](/en/locale/la-veranda-hotel-columbus)** remains
in the film, but the venue is no longer open: start from Palazzo Sacchetti and
the Janiculum.
