The **Tuscany** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* is a kind of
homecoming: as a child the actor lived for a year in Florence with his family.
Among the stops named in CNN's guide to the episode is an osteria where you
can still eat today.

## Osteria del Cinghiale Bianco

**[Osteria del Cinghiale Bianco](/en/locale/osteria-cinghiale-bianco-firenze)**
stands at **Borgo San Jacopo 43r**, in a 14th-century tower in the Oltrarno. The
Masselli family has run it since 1 March 1984. In the episode Tucci eats three
signature dishes of humble Tuscan cooking: **ribollita**, **pappa al pomodoro**
and **panzanella**.

## The market and the steak

In the same episode chef Fabio Picchi takes Tucci to Mercato di Sant'Ambrogio to
buy bistecca alla fiorentina, then cooks it with him. These are storytelling
moments, not venues we can list with a verified address.

## A Florentine itinerary

After lunch in the Oltrarno, the centre is within walking distance: Ponte
Vecchio, Piazza della Signoria and **[Caffè Rivoire](/en/locale/rivoire-firenze)**
from the film *Hannibal*. If you like cinema in a trattoria, there is also
**[Buca dell'Orafo](/en/locale/buca-dellorafo-firenze)** from *From Scratch* and
**[Trattoria Coco Lezzone](/en/blog/i-laureati-coco-lezzone-firenze)** from *I
laureati*. All the city's venues are on the **[Florence](/en/firenze)** page; for
the other episodes read *[Stanley Tucci in
Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*.
