**Naples pizzerias in films** aren't the ones tourist lists promise: after **L'Antica Pizzeria
da Michele**, the pizzeria of *Eat Pray Love*, many famous names have no provable link to
cinema. Here are the ones that really appear in a film or series, the ones that do so only by
tradition, and the addresses to plan your tour. It's the natural follow-up to
*[Eat Pray Love: Julia Roberts's pizzeria](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)*.

## Da Michele, Forcella: Eat Pray Love

It's the pizzeria of *Eat Pray Love*, a pairing reported by the Ministry of Culture's
Italy for Movies portal: here Liz (Julia Roberts) eats the margherita of the film's
most famous scene. You take a number at the door and you can't book. All the details (menu,
history, how to get there) are in the dedicated guide:
**[L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele](/en/locale/pizzeria-da-michele-napoli)**.

## Pizzaria La Notizia 53, Via Caravaggio

**Enzo Coccia** opened La Notizia in 1994 at **53 Via Michelangelo da Caravaggio**, away from
the tourist circuits. In the CNN series *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* Coccia makes Tucci
a classic Neapolitan pizza. It's a real venue with a link documented by the press: listing and
map at **[Pizzaria La Notizia 53](/en/locale/la-notizia-napoli)**.

## Starita in Materdei: Sophia Loren's fried pizza

**Starita** began in 1901 as a cellar in the **Materdei** district and in the 1940s became a
trattoria, pizzeria and fry shop, always family-run. It's famous for its link to Vittorio De
Sica's *The Gold of Naples* (*L'oro di Napoli*, 1954), where Sophia Loren fries pizzas. Many
guides say the scene was shot here, but Scatti di Gusto reports that some Neapolitans dispute
the identification: for them the film's shop was elsewhere. Until sources converge we keep it
as a **venue tied to the film**, not a set.

- **Address:** 27/28 Via Materdei, 80136 Naples ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pizzeria+Starita+Materdei+Napoli)).
- **Hours:** [TO VERIFY: one source says closed on Monday, another open every day].
- **Booking:** one guide notes there are no bookings and advises arriving at 12:30 or 7:30 pm
  to avoid the queue.

## Brandi and the others: why they're not on the list

**Pizzeria Brandi**, on Salita Sant'Anna di Palazzo near Via Toledo, is the "Margherita
pizzeria": by tradition, in 1889 Raffaele Esposito made the pizza dedicated to the queen here.
Gambero Rosso calls the story a legend: the documents don't mention pizza. On the cinema side I
found no source tying it to a film, and its founding date changes by source (1738, 1760 or
1780). The press also reported a NAS closure in October 2022, for two days: [TO VERIFY current
status]. For Sorbillo and Di Matteo I found no link with a film, so we don't present them as
cinema venues.

## How to plan the tour

1. **Forcella**: Da Michele, on foot from Napoli Centrale (under 10 minutes).
2. **Materdei**: Starita, by metro (Materdei stop) or taxi.
3. **Via Toledo**: Brandi, if you want the legend stop.
4. **Via Caravaggio**: La Notizia 53, for the pizza from Tucci's episode.
