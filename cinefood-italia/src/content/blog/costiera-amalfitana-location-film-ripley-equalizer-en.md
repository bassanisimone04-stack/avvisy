Few coastlines in the world have the cinematic density of the Amalfi Coast.
In recent years two major international productions have chosen the same
small village, **Atrani**, to tell very different stories — proof that this
stretch of coast still draws Hollywood as much as it did in the Dolce Vita
years.

## Ripley (Netflix, 2024): Atrani as the early-1960s Amalfi Coast

Netflix's *[Ripley](/en/blog/ripley-netflix-atrani-costiera-amalfitana)*,
starring **Andrew Scott** and winner of two 2024 Emmys, chose Atrani to
recreate the early-1960s Amalfi Coast (the series is set in 1961) from Patricia Highsmith's novel. It's a
different adaptation from
*[The Talented Mr. Ripley](/en/blog/mr-ripley-venezia-caffe-florian)*
(1999) with Matt Damon, shot instead on Ischia, Procida, in Rome and Venice: same book, different
settings.

## The Equalizer 3 (2023): Atrani becomes "Altamonte"

A year earlier, Antoine Fuqua had chosen the same village for
*[The Equalizer 3](/en/blog/equalizer-3-atrani-ristorante-savo)*, starring
**Denzel Washington**. In the film Atrani is disguised as a fictional town,
"Altamonte": the production replaced the signs on almost every shop around
the square, turning the real
**[Ristorante Savò](/en/locale/ristorante-savo-atrani)** into the "Aminah's
Cafe" where the lead orders his tea every day.

## Why Atrani specifically

At barely 0.12 km² (about 12 hectares), Atrani is **Italy's smallest municipality** by
area: one little square, covered alleys, narrow staircases between the
houses. It's a few minutes' walk from Amalfi, but far quieter — one of the
reasons two different productions chose it within the space of a single
year, over the better-known and busier Positano and Amalfi.

## Positano: Hotel Costiera

A little further west, **Positano** stars in the series *Hotel Costiera*
(2025): read the details in our article on
**[Villa Treville and the real Positano](/en/blog/hotel-costiera-positano-villa-treville)**.

## An itinerary along the coast

Atrani, Amalfi and Positano follow one another along the SS163, the classic
scenic coast road: all three can be visited in a single day, by car or on
the SITA buses, though summer traffic can stretch travel times
considerably. Atrani in particular has no parking in its old town, which is
entirely pedestrian.
