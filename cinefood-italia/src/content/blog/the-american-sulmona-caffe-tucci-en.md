In 2009 Sulmona woke up to find George Clooney under its arcades. Anton
Corbijn's *The American* is a slow, near-silent thriller shot almost
entirely in the province of L'Aquila: the story of a gunsmith and
assassin who takes refuge in a mountain village after an ambush.

## Where The American was filmed: Sulmona and Abruzzo

The Abruzzo Region Film Commission documents the locations: besides
**Sulmona**, the film shot in **Castel del Monte**, **Pacentro**,
**Castelvecchio Calvisio**, **Calascio**, **Corfinio** and **Campo
Imperatore**. A catalogue of the region's most photogenic villages and
high plateaus, used not as postcards but as empty space for Clooney's
character to disappear into.

## Four days on Piazza Garibaldi

The film's urban centre is Sulmona, and specifically **Piazza
Garibaldi**, the great market square dominated by the medieval aqueduct.
Filming lasted **four days**, with around **1,600 extras** and some
thirty businesses on the square taken over by the production.

Among them was **[Caffè Tucci](/en/locale/caffe-tucci-sulmona)**, a
family-run bar under the arcades. Owner Sante Tucci told the newspaper
*Il Centro* that he took down his outdoor gazebo to clear the way for the
camera: "We said yes right from the start, we're very happy about it."

> As always, we stop where the sources do: the venue's involvement in the
> shoot is documented; which specific scene was filmed there is not.

## What to see in Sulmona

Beyond the square and the aqueduct, the **Cathedral of San Panfilo**, the
**Santissima Annunziata** complex and the historic **confetti** factories
are all worth a stop — the sugared almonds that made Sulmona famous long
before cinema did. The town is also a good base for the Majella National
Park.

## In short

*The American* put Abruzzo's hill towns on screens worldwide, and Piazza
Garibaldi is still the best place to find that atmosphere: a coffee under
the arcades, the aqueduct opposite, the mountains all around. Discover
more **[films and shows](/en/film)** shot in Italy.
