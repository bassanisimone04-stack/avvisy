Pixar's **Luca** made the whole world dream of the **Italian Riviera**. The
fictional town of Portorosso, with its colourful alleys and crystal-clear sea, is
inspired by the Cinque Terre and the villages of Liguria. And it is a film in
which food is everywhere: perfect for a tasty family trip.

## The Liguria of Luca

Portorosso is Liguria in a nutshell: pastel houses, boats, little squares and the
scent of the sea. The villages that inspired it — Riomaggiore, Vernazza,
Monterosso — are among the most photographed in Italy.

## What to eat (just like in the film)

In the film they eat mountains of pasta: impossible not to mention **trofie al
pesto**, Liguria's signature dish. Also try:

- **Genoese focaccia**, tall and just oily enough;
- **Trenette** and **pansoti** with walnut sauce;
- Chickpea **farinata**;
- **Gelato**, the absolute star of Luca's summer days.

## A family itinerary

The Cinque Terre and the Ligurian coast are ideal for a weekend of sea, villages
and good food, suitable for children who grew up with the film. Scenic trains,
trails and little beaches complete the picture.

## In short

*Luca* is Liguria set to animation: colours, sea and pesto. A film that makes you
hungry and eager to travel. Discover the other **[films](/en/film)** and their
food locations.
