Winner of two **Emmy Awards** in 2024 (including Best Directing), Netflix's
**Ripley** miniseries brought Patricia Highsmith's novel back to the screen,
filmed entirely in Italy in elegant black and white. The absolute star: the
coast of **Atrani**, on the Amalfi Coast.

## Where Ripley was filmed: Atrani, the heart of the series

**Atrani**, the smallest municipality in Italy, in the province of Salerno,
becomes the town where protagonist Tom Ripley reaches Dickie Greenleaf. The
narrow, steep alleys of the historic centre, overlooking the sea, form the
backdrop to the early episodes. Dickie's villa, however, was filmed in
**Capri**.

## The other Italian locations

The series crosses Italy: **Naples** (in the Rione Sanità, between vico
Cimitile and via Fonseca), **Venice** (Palazzo Contarini Polignac, St Mark's
Square), **Rome**, **Florence** and **Palermo**. A true journey through 1960s
Italy.

## What to eat on the Amalfi Coast

The cuisine of Atrani and the coast is a burst of Mediterranean flavours:
**scialatielli with seafood**, **lemon delight cake**, **sfusato amalfitano**
(the local PGI lemon) and fresh Tyrrhenian fish. To complete the Campania
itinerary, the series' Naples stop connects to our guide on
**[L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)**.
