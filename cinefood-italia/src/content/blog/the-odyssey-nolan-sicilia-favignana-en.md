Released in summer 2026, **The Odyssey** by Christopher Nolan is already one of
the films of the year. The epic, based on Homer's poem, chose **Sicily** as the
setting for the island of Ithaca and the mythical lands of Ulysses' journey.
Here is where it was filmed and what to put on the table.

## Favignana, the film's Ithaca

The main set is **Favignana**, the largest of the Egadi Islands off Sicily's
western coast. The exterior of the palace of Ithaca is the
**Castello di Santa Caterina**, a 15th-century fortress atop the mountain of the
same name, with a 360-degree view over the Mediterranean.

## The Aeolian Islands as Aeolia

Off Sicily's northern coast, the volcanic **Aeolian Islands** — Vulcano, Lipari
and Basiluzzo — became the mythical island of Aeolia. The
**Faraglioni di Lipari**, spectacular rock formations, are the backdrop for the scene where Odysseus is tied to the ship's mast.

## What to eat on Favignana and in the Egadi Islands

Favignana is the home of **bluefin tuna**, tied to the centuries-old tradition
of the **mattanza**. Try tuna in every form (raw, grilled, preserved), fish
couscous (a legacy of nearby Tunisia) and the white wines of the Egadi Islands.
For the flavours of the Aeolian Islands, capers and Malvasia remain the stars —
we cover them in our itinerary
**[Il Postino in Salina](/en/blog/il-postino-salina-eolie)**.
