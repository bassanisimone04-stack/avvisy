**The Odyssey**, Christopher Nolan's film released on 17 July 2026, was shot largely in
Sicily: **Favignana**, in the Egadi Islands, for Ithaca, and the **Aeolian Islands** for
the sirens' sea. If you search **The Odyssey filming locations**, here are the places
sources confirm, what to verify before you travel and where to really eat in Favignana.

## The Odyssey locations at a glance

- **Favignana** (Egadi): Santa Caterina Castle, Cala Rossa, Cala Azzurra, Cala del Bue
  Marino.
- **Lipari** (Aeolians): the Sirens scene, according to Il Post, with a base at Pignataro
  harbour.
- **Vulcano and Basiluzzo**: named by several guides among the film's Sicilian islands.
- **Outside Italy**: Morocco, Iceland and Scotland, according to the international press.

The main shoot, according to Focus, ran between February and August 2025.

## Favignana, the film's Ithaca

**Favignana** is shaped like a butterfly and has a heart of tuff quarries. Here the production
chose **Santa Caterina Castle** as Odysseus's home in Ithaca. Sky TG24 reported that many
Favignana residents were picked as extras. Sources disagree on timing: one says about a month
from March 2025, ANSA about eight weeks in the Egadi. For the coves, one preview names
**Cala Rossa**, **Cala Azzurra** and **Cala del Bue Marino**.

### Santa Caterina Castle: how to get there

The fort stands on top of Monte Santa Caterina, at about 310 metres, on the site of a
watchtower: local tradition speaks of Saracen origins and of use as a Bourbon prison. Today it
is **abandoned but reachable on foot**. There are no official opening hours: (to be verified: with
Favignana's town hall or the tourist office). Guides advise closed shoes, full daylight and
stable weather, and a torch for the interiors. From the top you see the quarries, the island's
«butterfly» and, on clear days, the other Egadi.

## The Aeolians: Lipari, Vulcano and Basiluzzo

In the Aeolians the production shot in **Lipari** from 21 April 2025 (Il Post), with the scene
of Odysseus meeting the **Sirens**. According to ENI (THE VIEW), the Sirens trial is set near
**Pietra del Bagno**, while **Pietra Lunga** and **Pietra Menalda** are the voyage's dangerous
rocks. **Vulcano** and **Basiluzzo** appear in several guides. There is no official scene-by-scene
list: the matches rest on press reports. For the flavours of Salina and the Aeolians read
*[Il Postino in Salina](/en/blog/il-postino-salina-eolie)*.

## How to get to Favignana

From **Trapani** Liberty Lines hydrofoils take about 30–40 minutes and Siremar ferries about
an hour; from **Marsala** mostly hydrofoils run. Only Siremar carries vehicles, and hydrofoils
limit hand luggage. Runs increase in summer and one-way prices start around €12: (to be verified: on Liberty Lines and Siremar).

## Where to eat in Favignana

Favignana is the home of **red tuna**. To see why, visit the **Ex Stabilimento Florio delle
Tonnare**, at **29 Via Amendola** ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ex+Stabilimento+Florio+Favignana)). Entry is only
with paid guided visits (€8 full according to one listing), but sources' hours disagree: call
+39 338 5365899 (to be verified).

For a meal, three addresses guides mention (none tied to the film):

- **La Bettola**: a historic family-run restaurant, known for **fish couscous**, at 47 Via
  Nicotera (the address also appears for another venue: (to be verified)).
- **Quello che c'è... c'è!**: Via Garibaldi, in the centre; spaghetti with sea urchin, fish
  couscous and tuna carpaccio.
- **Osteria Cavallo**: traditional cooking, outside the centre; address (to be verified).

Another thing to look for on menus: **frascatole**, similar to couscous.
