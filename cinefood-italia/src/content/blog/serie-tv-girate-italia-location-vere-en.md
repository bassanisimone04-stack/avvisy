Television has become one of the most powerful engines of film tourism in
Italy. From Sky productions to Netflix and HBO, more and more international
shows are choosing Italian cities and villages as genuine sets — not just
backdrops, but places you can actually go and visit.

## The White Lotus — Taormina, Sicily

Season 2 of the HBO series made **Taormina** one of the most searched
destinations of 2022-2023. Discover the real locations in our article on
**[The White Lotus and the real Sicily](/en/blog/the-white-lotus-sicilia-taormina)**.

## Ripley — Amalfi Coast

The Netflix miniseries, winner of two Emmys, chose **Atrani** to recreate
the 1950s Amalfi Coast of Patricia Highsmith's novel. Details in our
**[deep dive on Ripley](/en/blog/ripley-netflix-atrani-costiera-amalfitana)**.

## Inspector Montalbano — Ragusa and Punta Secca

For over twenty years, the Rai series based on Camilleri's novels has made
**Punta Secca**, **Scicli** and **Ragusa Ibla** famous. The inspector is
also a food lover: discover
**[where Montalbano ate](/en/blog/dove-mangiava-montalbano-sicilia)**.

## My Brilliant Friend — Naples

The HBO/Rai series based on Elena Ferrante's novels mixes a built set in
Marcianise with real Naples streets, including the historic Galleria
Principe. Read **[the real Naples behind the set](/en/blog/amica-geniale-napoli-scottojonno)**.

## Il Re — Trieste

The Sky Original series starring Luca Zingaretti filmed across Rome, Turin
and **Trieste**, where it also used the interiors of the Antico Caffè San
Marco. Discover **[Il Re in Trieste](/en/blog/il-re-trieste-caffe-san-marco)**.

## Rocco Schiavone — Aosta

Based on Antonio Manzini's novels, the Rai series filmed in **Aosta**,
where a historic café was reopened just for the shoot. Read
**[the story of Caffè Nazionale](/en/blog/rocco-schiavone-aosta-caffe-nazionale)**.

## Succession — Tuscany

The season 3 finale wedding of the HBO series about the Roy family is shot
between Villa La Foce and Villa Cetinale, in the Val d'Orcia. Discover
**[the real Tuscany of Succession](/en/blog/succession-toscana-villa-la-foce)**.

## Master of None — Modena

Season 2 of the Netflix series created by Aziz Ansari opens in **Modena**,
between a fresh pasta lesson and a dinner at Osteria Francescana. Read
**[Modena and Osteria Francescana](/en/blog/master-of-none-modena-osteria-francescana)**.

## Blocco 181 – Gangs of Milano — Turin

The Sky series created by Salmo is set in Milan but was filmed almost
entirely in **Turin**: Ristorante Del Cambio is among the more than a
hundred Piedmont locations used for season 2. Discover **[Blocco 181 in
Turin](/en/blog/blocco-181-torino-del-cambio)**.

## The Red Door — Trieste

The Rai noir starring Lino Guanciale and Gabriella Pession filmed for
three seasons in Trieste, with the Antico Caffè San Marco among its
recurring locations. Discover **[The Red Door and Caffè San
Marco](/en/blog/la-porta-rossa-trieste-caffe-san-marco)**.

## Commissioner Ricciardi — Naples

Set in 1930s Naples, the Rai series starring Lino Guanciale filmed in
authentic city locations, including the **Gran Caffè Gambrinus**,
transformed into the period café the commissioner visits. Discover
**[the real Gambrinus](/en/blog/commissario-ricciardi-napoli-gambrinus)**.

## Don Matteo — Gubbio, Umbria

For thirteen seasons, the longest-running Italian crime-comedy series used
**Gubbio** as its fixed set: Caffè Ducale, on Piazza Grande, is still the
café where Don Matteo and Sergeant Cecchini play their chess games.
Discover **[Don Matteo in Gubbio](/en/blog/don-matteo-gubbio-caffe-ducale)**.

## Anthony Bourdain: No Reservations — Trastevere, Rome

In the Rome episode of the Travel Channel culinary travel series,
Anthony Bourdain finds his favorite cacio e pepe at Ristorante Roma
Sparita, initially keeping its name secret. Discover
**[Trastevere's secret trattoria](/en/blog/bourdain-roma-sparita-trastevere)**.

## Màkari — Scopello, Sicily

In the Rai series starring Claudio Gioè, Marilù's restaurant is a set
built inside the old **Tonnara di Scopello**, overlooking the sea stacks
between Castellammare del Golfo and San Vito Lo Capo. Discover
**[the real tonnara](/en/blog/makari-tonnara-scopello)**.

## In short

From the Sicily of *The White Lotus* to the Aosta Valley of *Rocco
Schiavone*, international TV series keep choosing Italy as their set — and
leaving behind real locations open to the public. Discover every
**[TV show](/en/film)** in our directory, or the full list of
**[restaurants seen on screen](/en/blog/ristoranti-veri-nei-film-italia-lista)**.
