Among Italian films that chose a real restaurant as a location, *La moglie del
prete* (1970) by **Dino Risi**, with **Sophia Loren** and **Marcello Mastroianni**,
is one of the lesser-known. The **Italy for Movies** portal, linked to Italy's Ministry
of Culture, lists the **[restaurant Al Zocco](/en/locale/al-zocco-grisignano)**
in **Grisignano di Zocco**, in the province of Vicenza, among the locations.

## The restaurant

Al Zocco is at **Largo Galileo Galilei 2** and is still in business. The secondary
sources we consulted name it as the place of the two lovers' lunch, played by Mastroianni
and Loren. Italy for Movies confirms the venue as a location but does not describe the
scene: that is why we treat it with caution.

## The film

*[La moglie del prete](/en/film/la-moglie-del-prete)* is a 1970 film, shot in Vicenza
and its surroundings. We don't add plot details we couldn't verify.
