In *Spider-Man: Far From Home*, Peter Parker begins his European trip right in
**Venice**, where he faces the water monster among canals and bridges. The lagoon
city, star of one of the film's most spectacular sequences, is also a surprising
food destination. Here is how to experience it.

## The real and the rebuilt Venice

Between the Grand Canal, the little squares and the palaces on the water,
Venice offers unique scenery. The crew genuinely filmed in the city in
2018, in spots like **Campo Santa Maria Formosa**, the **Rialto** area and
**St Mark's Square**. For scale and safety reasons, though, the
spectacular action sequence with the water monster was rebuilt on a huge
set at **Leavesden Studios** in England (the same studios used for Harry
Potter), with the boat scene shot against green screen on a soundstage. A
mix of real locations and special effects that still captures the real
city's atmosphere faithfully.

## Where to eat like a Venetian

Forget the tourist restaurants: the real Venice is enjoyed in the **bacari**, the
small taverns where you do the "giro di ombre". Try:

- **Cicchetti**, Venetian snacks (creamed cod, sarde in saor, meatballs);
- A **spritz** or a small glass of wine ("ombra");
- **Bigoli in salsa** and **risotto di gò**;
- For a "cinematic" coffee, the historic
  **[Caffè Florian](/en/locale/caffe-florian)** in St Mark's Square.

Discover the venues of **[Venice](/en/venezia)** on our site.

## An itinerary between alleys and canals

Getting lost on foot among the sestieri, away from the tourist routes, is the best
way to experience the Venice of the film. Every bridge is a potential shot.

## In short

*Spider-Man: Far From Home* shows a superhero Venice: spectacular and suspended
over the water. Experience it between a cicchetto and a spritz. Browse the other
**[films](/en/film)** set in the lagoon.
