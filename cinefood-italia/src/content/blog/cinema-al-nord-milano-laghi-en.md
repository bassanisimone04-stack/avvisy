Not only Rome and Sicily: **Northern Italy** too has a long history of film sets,
between metropolitan elegance, lakes and countryside.

## Milan, between fashion and cinema

The chic Milan of the Brera district and its palaces is the backdrop of
*House of Gucci* by Ridley Scott, with the fashion dynasty at the centre of the
scene. The city combines design, aperitivo and great cinema.

## Crema and Guadagnino's lowlands

Not far away, the Crema countryside is the heart of *Call Me by Your Name*: the
squares and arcades of **Crema** offer the suspended atmosphere of an
unforgettable summer.
