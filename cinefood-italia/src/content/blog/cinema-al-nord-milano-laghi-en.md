Not only Rome and Sicily: **Northern Italy** too has a long history of major
international film sets, between metropolitan elegance, lakes and
countryside.

## Milan, between fashion and cinema

In Milan, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and Villa Necchi Campiglio are the backdrop of
*House of Gucci* by Ridley Scott (2021), with the fashion dynasty at the
centre of the scene — while the scenes in the family's lake villa were
filmed at Villa Balbiano, on Lake Como. The city combines design,
aperitivo and great cinema.

## Crema and Guadagnino's lowlands

Not far away, the Crema countryside is the heart of *Call Me by Your
Name*, winner of the Oscar for James Ivory's screenplay: the squares and
arcades of **Crema** offer the suspended atmosphere of an unforgettable
summer.

## Lake Como, an international star

**Villa del Balbianello**, in Lenno, is among the most photographed film
locations in history: it hosted both James Bond's recovery in *Casino
Royale* and Anakin and Padmé's first kiss in *Star Wars: Episode II*. It's
open to the public from mid-March to November.

## What to eat between Milan and the lakes

Northern cuisine is hearty and refined: in Milan, **saffron risotto** and
**veal cutlet**; on Lake Como, **risotto with perch** and **missoltini**,
and from nearby Valtellina, **pizzoccheri**. An itinerary that
combines metropolis and nature, without ever giving up good food.
