Sicily is a land of cinema and flavours. Among baroque villages and crystalline
sea, many places on the island have become unforgettable sets — and perfect
occasions to eat like in the movies.

## The Godfather, between Savoca and Forza d'Agrò

The "Sicilian" scenes of *The Godfather* were shot in the villages of the
Messina area. At **Bar Vitelli** in Savoca, Michael Corleone asks for
Apollonia's hand: the place is still almost identical to 1972.

## Montalbano's cuisine, in the Ragusa area

The series *Inspector Montalbano* made Punta Secca, Scicli and Ragusa Ibla
famous. The inspector is also a gourmet: on the seafront of **Punta Secca** you
can enjoy the seafood dishes he loves so much.

## The magic of Cinema Paradiso

In **Palazzo Adriano**, the beautiful Piazza Umberto I is the heart of
*Cinema Paradiso*, Tornatore's Oscar winner: a village where time seems to have
stopped.
