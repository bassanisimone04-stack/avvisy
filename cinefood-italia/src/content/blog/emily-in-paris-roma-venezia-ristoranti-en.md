With season 5 (2025), *Emily in Paris* added a new Italian chapter to its
map: Emily moves to run the agency's Rome office, and the show delivers a
genuine tour of **Rome**'s most exclusive restaurants, before closing the
season in **Venice**.

## The real restaurants of Rome

Unlike many productions, the venues here are real and individually
identifiable: **Zuma**, a high-end Japanese restaurant inside Palazzo
Fendi with a view of the Spanish Steps; **Settimo**, on the rooftop
terrace of the Sofitel hotel on Via Lombardia; **Il Giardino Ristorante**,
the rooftop of Hotel Eden; and the back garden of **Hotel de Russie**, one
of Rome's most photographed spots. Even the agency's Rome offices were
filmed in a real location: the historic **Fendi** headquarters, the 1940s
rationalist building known as the "Square Colosseum".

## The Venice finale

The last episode, titled *Veni, Vidi, Venezia*, takes the characters to
Venice Fashion Week. Filmed between August 5–15, 2025, it features **St
Mark's Square**, **Campo Santa Maria Nova** and the church of **San
Francesco della Vigna**, where the fictional fashion show is held. For
lodging, the show uses real hotels like the **Hotel Danieli** and the
**St. Regis Venice**.

## A luxury itinerary, between Rome and Venice

Retracing Emily's itinerary means alternating rooftop aperitivos over
Rome's skyline with strolls along Venice's canals: a route through some of
Italy's most exclusive addresses, from historic hotels to rooftop bars.
