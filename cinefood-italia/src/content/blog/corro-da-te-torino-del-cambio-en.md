Riccardo Milani's *Corro da te* (*Running to You*) is a romantic comedy
that uses Turin as an elegant backdrop. Shot in the city in February 2020
and released in cinemas in March 2022, it pairs Pierfrancesco Favino, a
serial seducer pretending to use a wheelchair, with Miriam Leone, a
paraplegic musician who changes how he sees himself.

## Turin, the film's city

Filming took place with the support of the **Film Commission Torino
Piemonte**, which documented the locations. The couple's route runs
between two landmarks of royal Turin a short walk apart: the **Teatro
Regio**, where Chiara performs, and **Ristorante Del Cambio**, where the
two go for dinner straight afterwards.

## Dinner at Del Cambio

**[Del Cambio](/en/locale/del-cambio-torino)** has been open since
**1757** on Piazza Carignano, opposite the Teatro Carignano: one of the
oldest historic restaurants in Italy still in business. Its 19th-century
rooms — mirrors, gilded stucco, red velvet — are unchanged. Today the restaurant holds **a Michelin
star** and serves contemporary Piedmontese cuisine.

In the scene, as the Italian locations portal describes it, Gianni takes
Chiara to dinner after the concert and the couple dine amused by his
clumsiness — a light moment that is also where the character's pretence
starts to crack.

## An evening following the film

Piazza Carignano is the heart of stately Turin: Piazza Castello, the
Egyptian Museum and the Galleria Subalpina are all minutes away on foot.
Anyone wanting to retrace the film's route can pair a performance at the
**Teatro Regio** with dinner at Del Cambio — the two are less than ten
minutes apart. The restaurant is closed on Mondays and booking is
recommended.

## In short

Few Italian cities have a restaurant that tells their story the way Del
Cambio tells Turin's. *Corro da te* made it the stage for a dinner, and
that stage is still there, open every evening. Discover more **[films and
