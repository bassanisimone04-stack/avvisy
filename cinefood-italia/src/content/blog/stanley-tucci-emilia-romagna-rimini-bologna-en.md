The **Bologna** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* portrays Emilia-Romagna
with an exceptional guide, chef **Massimo Bottura**, who has three Michelin stars.
Here are the places in the episode, separating those we can list from those that
remain only a television memory.

## Rimini: Osteria io e Simone

Tucci ends the journey in **Rimini**, Federico Fellini's hometown, with the
director's niece, **Francesca Fellini**. The meal is at **[Osteria io e
Simone](/en/locale/osteria-io-e-simone-rimini)**, at **Piazzetta Teatini 3**: a small
family-run venue where they taste handmade cappelletti and strozzapreti.

## Casa Artusi, Forlimpopoli

The episode also features **[Casa Artusi](/en/locale/casa-artusi-forlimpopoli)**, the
centre of food culture dedicated to Pellegrino Artusi in his hometown,
Forlimpopoli.

## Bologna: market, delis and charity kitchens

In Bologna Tucci visits the **Quadrilatero** market and **Salumeria Simoni**, at Via
Drapperie 5/2a, open since 1960, where he marvels at the mortadella. Then he
watches the cooks of **Cucine Popolari** make tortellini. A dairy near Modena,
**Caseificio Rosola**, shows him Parmigiano Reggiano. Salumeria Simoni is a deli
rather than a venue with tables, and the others are production or charity places:
that is why we haven't listed them as places to eat.

## What to eat in Emilia-Romagna, like Tucci

**Tortellini**, **cappelletti**, **strozzapreti**, **mortadella** and **Parmigiano
Reggiano** are the dishes that come out of the episode. For more Emilian film and TV
places read *[Master of None in Modena](/en/blog/master-of-none-modena-osteria-francescana)*
and *[Somebody Feed Phil in Modena](/en/blog/somebody-feed-phil-modena-osteria-francescana)*.
For the other episodes: *[Stanley Tucci in Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*.
