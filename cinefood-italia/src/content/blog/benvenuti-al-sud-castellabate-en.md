*Welcome to the South* (*Benvenuti al Sud*) led millions of Italians to discover
**Castellabate**, a jewel of Cilento overlooking the sea. Luca Miniero's
box-office hit made its little square, its alleys and — of course — its cuisine
famous. Here is the itinerary through the locations and the good food.

## The film's locations

The heart is the **little square of Castellabate** (Piazza 10 Ottobre 1123),
where the film imagines the post office of the main character Alberto (Claudio
Bisio). Don't miss the **Belvedere di San Costabile** with its view over the
gulf, and the famous "You don't die here" plaque in Piazza Nicola Perrotti. The
final scenes were shot along the sea, towards the Torre Perrotti.

## Where to eat: Le Gatte and the little square

The scene where Alberto discovers the flavours of the South is set at the
**[Le Gatte restaurant](/en/locale/ristorante-le-gatte)**, in Santa Maria di
Castellabate, in a historic building overlooking the sea: Mediterranean cuisine,
fresh fish and Neapolitan pizza. For a coffee or an aperitivo in the film's
square, the stop is the
**[Bar La Piazzetta](/en/locale/bar-la-piazzetta-castellabate)**.

## The flavours of Cilento

This is the land of the **Mediterranean diet**: try the Cilento **buffalo
mozzarella**, menaica anchovies, hand-rolled fusilli and local extra-virgin olive
oil. The Tyrrhenian fish does the rest.

You will find all the connected stops on the
**[Welcome to the South](/en/film/benvenuti-al-sud)** page and the venues of
**[Castellabate](/en/castellabate)**.

## In short

Castellabate combines sea, village and generous cuisine: retracing the *Welcome to
the South* itinerary means sitting down at the
**[Le Gatte restaurant](/en/locale/ristorante-le-gatte)** and understanding why,
truly, "you don't die here".
