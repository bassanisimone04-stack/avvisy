*Welcome to the South* (*Benvenuti al Sud*, 2010), the Italian remake of
the French film **Welcome to the Sticks** (*Bienvenue chez les Ch'tis*),
led millions of Italians to discover **Castellabate**, a jewel of Cilento
overlooking the sea. **Luca Miniero**'s box-office hit grossed about
**€30 million** (it opened at number one at the Italian box office, ahead
of *Inception*), and made its little square, its alleys and — of course —
its cuisine famous. Here is the itinerary through the locations and the
good food.

## The film's locations

The heart is the **little square of Castellabate** (Piazza 10 Ottobre 1123),
where the film imagines the post office of the main character Alberto (Claudio
Bisio). Don't miss the **Belvedere di San Costabile** with its view over the
gulf, and the famous "You don't die here" plaque in Piazza Nicola Perrotti. The
final scenes were shot along the sea, towards the Torre Perrotti.

## Where to eat: Le Gatte and the little square

The dinner among colleagues is set on the **Porto delle Gatte**, in Santa Maria di
Castellabate, where the **[Le Gatte restaurant](/en/locale/ristorante-le-gatte)**
stands, overlooking the sea: Mediterranean cuisine and fresh fish. We list it as
*linked to the film*: we found no source saying the scene was shot in its dining
room. Read the full story in **[Le Gatte and the Porto delle Gatte](/en/blog/le-gatte-castellabate-benvenuti-al-sud)**.
For a coffee in the film's square you can stop at the
**[Bar La Piazzetta](/en/locale/bar-la-piazzetta-castellabate)**, a bar on the square
(we found no source naming it as a location).

## The flavours of Cilento

This is the land of the **Mediterranean diet**: try the Cilento **buffalo
mozzarella**, menaica anchovies, hand-rolled fusilli and local extra-virgin olive
oil. The Tyrrhenian fish does the rest.

You will find all the connected stops on the
**[Welcome to the South](/en/film/benvenuti-al-sud)** page and the venues of
**[Castellabate](/en/castellabate)**.

## In short

Castellabate combines sea, village and generous cuisine: retracing the *Welcome to
the South* itinerary means sitting down at the
**[Le Gatte restaurant](/en/locale/ristorante-le-gatte)** and understanding why,
truly, "you don't die here".
