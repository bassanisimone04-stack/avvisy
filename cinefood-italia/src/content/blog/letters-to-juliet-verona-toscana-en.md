*Letters to Juliet* (2010) is a romantic comedy that crosses two postcard Italies:
**Verona**, the city of Romeo and Juliet, and the **hills of Tuscany**, among
vineyards and wineries. A perfect itinerary for lovers of cinema and good wine.

## The Verona of Juliet

The film starts at the famous **House of Juliet**, with its balcony and the wall
covered in love letters. Verona is an elegant, romantic city, with the Arena, the
squares and the medieval palaces.

## The flavours of Verona

Verona's cuisine is rich and tied to wine:

- **Amarone risotto**, creamy and fragrant;
- **Bigoli** with duck ragù;
- **Pastissada de caval** and **lesso con la pearà**;
- The great wines of Valpolicella: **Amarone**, Valpolicella, Recioto.

## The Tuscany of the film

The second part moves among the Tuscan hills and wineries, between tastings and
golden panoramas. Here the **bistecca alla fiorentina**, **pici** and red wines
(Chianti, Brunello) reign.

## In short

*Letters to Juliet* combines romance and taste, from Verona to Tuscany. An ideal
journey between love, cinema and glasses of wine. Discover the other
**[films](/en/film)** and their food locations.
