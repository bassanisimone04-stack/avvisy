In Italian cinema food is never just food: it is story, identity, comedy and
nostalgia. Here are some of the dining scenes that left a mark.

## The ritual of Roman cuisine

*An American in Rome* gave us one of the most famous scenes ever, with pasta as
the star: an ironic hymn to the capital's working-class cuisine.

## Conviviality as storytelling

From *Welcome to the South*, with its dinners among colleagues, to the culinary
discovery of *Eat Pray Love*: the table becomes the place where characters truly
meet.

## Elegance and nostalgia

The refined dinners of *The Great Beauty* and the timeless cafés tell of an Italy
suspended between beauty and melancholy.

Want to live these atmospheres? Discover the [venues](/en/locali) where these
scenes were shot or set. *Happy viewing — and buon appetito!*
