Some restaurants have been made more famous by cinema than by the stars who
walked through them. Here is the full list of **real, verified** venues that
genuinely appeared in a film or TV series — no themed sets, just addresses
that still exist today, alongside the actor whose scene made them famous.

## Al Pacino — Bar Vitelli, Savoca (Sicily)

In the village of Savoca, **[Bar Vitelli](/en/locale/bar-vitelli-savoca)** is
where Michael Corleone asks for Apollonia's hand in
*[The Godfather](/en/blog/bar-vitelli-savoca-padrino)* (1972). The venue
didn't even have an official name before the film.

## Julia Roberts — L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele, Naples

In the Forcella district, **[Da Michele](/en/locale/pizzeria-da-michele-napoli)**
is the pizzeria where Liz tastes her first real margherita in
*[Eat Pray Love](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)* (2010) —
one of the most quoted food scenes in recent cinema.

## Matt Damon and Jude Law — Caffè Florian, Venice

On St Mark's Square, **[Caffè Florian](/en/locale/caffe-florian)**, Italy's
oldest café (since 1720), is where Tom Ripley meets Marge and Dickie's
father in *[The Talented Mr. Ripley](/en/blog/mr-ripley-venezia-caffe-florian)*
(1999).

## Denzel Washington — Ristorante Savò, Atrani

On the square of Italy's smallest municipality,
**[Ristorante Savò](/en/locale/ristorante-savo-atrani)** becomes Robert
McCall's "Aminah's Cafe" in
*[The Equalizer 3](/en/blog/equalizer-3-atrani-ristorante-savo)* (2023) —
the venue tells the story itself on its own website.

## Lady Gaga and Adam Driver — Milan

The locations of *[House of Gucci](/en/blog/house-of-gucci-milano)* (2021)
run through the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele and the heart of Milan's fashion
district, between elegance and ambition.

## Anthony Hopkins — Caffè Rivoire, Florence

Facing Piazza della Signoria, **[Caffè Rivoire](/en/locale/rivoire-firenze)**
is among the Florence landmarks recognisable in
*[Hannibal](/en/blog/hannibal-firenze-caffe-rivoire)* (2001), the sequel to
*The Silence of the Lambs*.

## George Clooney — Caffè Tucci, Sulmona

On Piazza Garibaldi, **[Caffè Tucci](/en/locale/caffe-tucci-sulmona)** was
drawn into the production of
*[The American](/en/blog/the-american-sulmona-caffe-tucci)* (2010): the
owner took down his own outdoor gazebo to clear the way for the camera.

## Pierfrancesco Favino and Miriam Leone — Del Cambio, Turin

Open since 1757 on Piazza Carignano, **[Del Cambio](/en/locale/del-cambio-torino)**,
today a Michelin-starred restaurant, hosts the dinner scene between the two
leads of *[Corro da te](/en/blog/corro-da-te-torino-del-cambio)* (2022).

## Marco Giallini — Caffè Nazionale, Aosta

The historic café on Piazza Chanoux was reopened just to film
*[Rocco Schiavone](/en/blog/rocco-schiavone-aosta-caffe-nazionale)*: today
it's genuinely open again, under chef Paolo Griffa.

## Audrey Hepburn — Roman gelato

No list is complete without *Roman Holiday*: discover
**[where Audrey Hepburn ate gelato](/en/blog/vacanze-romane-gelato-roma)**
in 1950s Rome.
