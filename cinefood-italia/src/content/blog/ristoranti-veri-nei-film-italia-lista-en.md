Some restaurants have been made more famous by cinema than by the stars who
walked through them. Here is the list of **real** venues that, according to the
sources cited on each card, appeared in a film or TV series — no themed sets, just addresses
that still exist today, alongside the actor whose scene made them famous.

## Al Pacino: where The Godfather was filmed, Bar Vitelli in Savoca

In the village of Savoca, **[Bar Vitelli](/en/locale/bar-vitelli-savoca)** is
where Michael Corleone asks for Apollonia's hand in
*[The Godfather](/en/blog/bar-vitelli-savoca-padrino)* (1972). The venue
didn't even have an official name before the film.

## Julia Roberts — L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele, Naples

In the Forcella district, **[Da Michele](/en/locale/pizzeria-da-michele-napoli)**
is the pizzeria where Liz tastes her first real margherita in
*[Eat Pray Love](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)* (2010) —
one of the most quoted food scenes in recent cinema.

## Matt Damon — Caffè Florian, Venice

On St Mark's Square, **[Caffè Florian](/en/locale/caffe-florian)**, Italy's
oldest café (since 1720), is where Tom Ripley meets Marge and Dickie's
father in *[The Talented Mr. Ripley](/en/blog/mr-ripley-venezia-caffe-florian)*
(1999).

## Denzel Washington — Ristorante Savò, Atrani

On the square of Italy's smallest municipality,
**[Ristorante Savò](/en/locale/ristorante-savo-atrani)** becomes Robert
McCall's "Aminah's Cafe" in
*[The Equalizer 3](/en/blog/equalizer-3-atrani-ristorante-savo)* (2023) —
the venue tells the story itself on its own website.

## Lady Gaga and Adam Driver — Milan

*[House of Gucci](/en/blog/house-of-gucci-milano)* (2021) was shot, among
other places, at the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and Villa Necchi Campiglio,
but we know of no venue confirmed among its locations: here you only find the places.

## Anthony Hopkins — Caffè Rivoire, Florence

Facing Piazza della Signoria, **[Caffè Rivoire](/en/locale/rivoire-firenze)**
is among the Florence landmarks recognisable in
*[Hannibal](/en/blog/hannibal-firenze-caffe-rivoire)* (2001), the sequel to
*The Silence of the Lambs*.

## George Clooney — Caffè Tucci, Sulmona

On Piazza Garibaldi, **[Caffè Tucci](/en/locale/caffe-tucci-sulmona)** was
drawn into the production of
*[The American](/en/blog/the-american-sulmona-caffe-tucci)* (2010): the
owner took down his own outdoor gazebo to clear the way for the camera.

## Pierfrancesco Favino and Miriam Leone — Del Cambio, Turin

Open since 1757 on Piazza Carignano, **[Del Cambio](/en/locale/del-cambio-torino)**,
today a Michelin-starred restaurant, hosts the dinner scene between the two
leads of *[Corro da te](/en/blog/corro-da-te-torino-del-cambio)* (2022).

## Marco Giallini — Caffè Nazionale, Aosta

The historic café on Piazza Chanoux, closed for some time, was reopened to film
*[Rocco Schiavone](/en/blog/rocco-schiavone-aosta-caffe-nazionale)*: today
it's genuinely open again, under chef Paolo Griffa.

## Audrey Hepburn — Roman gelato

No list is complete without *Roman Holiday*: discover
**[where Audrey Hepburn ate gelato](/en/blog/vacanze-romane-gelato-roma)**
in 1950s Rome.

## Meryl Streep and Anne Hathaway — Caffè Parigi, Milan

**[Caffè Parigi](/en/locale/caffe-parigi-milano)**, inside
Palazzo Parigi Hotel, is in the hotel that hosted the cast of
*[The Devil Wears Prada 2](/en/blog/il-diavolo-veste-prada-2-dove-e-stato-girato)*
(2026): the hotel reports scenes shot in the lobby, at the café and outside.

## Zoe Saldana — Buca dell'Orafo, Florence

In the Netflix series *[From Scratch](/en/blog/from-scratch-firenze-buca-dellorafo)*,
based on a true story, the character Lino works as a chef at
**[Buca dell'Orafo](/en/locale/buca-dellorafo-firenze)**, a historic
Florentine trattoria near Ponte Vecchio.

## Lino Guanciale — Gran Caffè Gambrinus, Naples

In the Rai series *[Commissioner Ricciardi](/en/blog/commissario-ricciardi-napoli-gambrinus)*,
**[Gran Caffè Gambrinus](/en/locale/gran-caffe-gambrinus-napoli)**, a
historic café dating to 1860, becomes the period venue the lead
frequents — with a table permanently reserved after the show's success.

## Claudia Gerini and Carlo Verdone — Paradiso Terrestre, Rome

In the comedy *[Honeymoons](/en/blog/viaggi-di-nozze-roma-paradiso-terrestre)*
(1995), Ivano and Jessica's wedding reception is filmed at
**[Ristorante Paradiso Terrestre](/en/locale/ristorante-paradiso-terrestre-roma)**,
still specialising in weddings today.

## Giacomo Gianniotti and Valerio Mastandrea — Caffè San Marco, Trieste

In *[Diabolik - Ginko Attacks!](/en/blog/diabolik-ginko-trieste-caffe-san-marco)*
(2022), Trieste becomes the comic's Clerville: the **[Antico Caffè San
Marco](/en/locale/caffe-san-marco-trieste)** appears in a counter scene,
not far from the chase in Piazza della Borsa.

## Terence Hill and Nino Frassica — Caffè Ducale, Gubbio

For many seasons of the Rai series
*[Don Matteo](/en/blog/don-matteo-gubbio-caffe-ducale)*,
**[Caffè Ducale](/en/locale/caffe-ducale-gubbio)** in Gubbio was the café
where Don Matteo and Sergeant Cecchini played their chess games.

## Anthony Bourdain — Roma Sparita, Trastevere

In the Rome episode of *[No Reservations](/en/blog/bourdain-roma-sparita-trastevere)*
(2010), Anthony Bourdain discovers his favorite cacio e pepe at
**[Ristorante Roma Sparita](/en/locale/roma-sparita-trastevere)**,
initially keeping its name secret.

## The 1960s jet set — Harry's Bar, Via Veneto

A surviving symbol of Fellini's "dolce vita" (not a filming location), **[Harry's
Bar](/en/locale/harrys-bar-roma)** is still in business today on the
same Via Veneto portrayed in *[La Dolce Vita](/en/blog/harrys-bar-roma-dolce-vita)*.

## Stanley Tucci — from Rome to Nerano

In the CNN series *[Searching for Italy](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-searching-for-italy-tappe)*,
Stanley Tucci stops for carciofi alla giudia at
**[La Reginella](/en/locale/la-reginella-roma)**, Enzo Coccia's pizza at
**[La Notizia 53](/en/locale/la-notizia-napoli)**, spaghetti alla Nerano at
**[Lo Scoglio](/en/locale/lo-scoglio-nerano)** and the cooking school at
**[Casa Artusi](/en/locale/casa-artusi-forlimpopoli)**.

## Phil Rosenthal — Venice

In the Venice episode of
*[Somebody Feed Phil](/en/blog/somebody-feed-phil-venezia-ristoranti)*, Phil
Rosenthal wonders whether **[Al Covo](/en/locale/al-covo-venezia)** might be
the city's best restaurant, and tastes the pork chop with vinegar at
**[Vini da Arturo](/en/locale/vini-da-arturo-venezia)**.

## Antonella Attili — Tonnara di Scopello, Sicily

In the Rai series *[Màkari](/en/blog/makari-tonnara-scopello)*, Marilù's
restaurant is a set built inside the old **[Tonnara di
Scopello](/en/locale/tonnara-di-scopello)**, overlooking the famous sea
stacks.
