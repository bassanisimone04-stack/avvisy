**The trattorias of Italian comedy in Rome** are rarer than tourist sites suggest: behind
the famous names, serious sources document a real link to cinema for only a few venues.
Here are two, with address, map and what to verify before going. For the rest of Rome at
the movies read *[Rome in the movies: 5 film restaurants](/en/blog/roma-nel-cinema-ristoranti-da-film)*.

## Sora Lella, the trattoria on Tiber Island

**Sora Lella** is at **16 Via di Ponte Quattro Capi**, on **Tiber Island**
([map]({M}Trattoria+Sora+Lella+Roma)). It was founded in 1959 by **Elena Fabrizi**, known as
Sora Lella, sister of the actor **Aldo Fabrizi**, who became known as a cook and a TV face in
the 1960s. On screen she is the grandmother in *Bianco, rosso e Verdone* (1981), Carlo
Verdone's film: this is the link guides report. Today the family runs it, her grandchildren
according to Gambero Rosso.

Dishes guides mention: **«Nonna Lella» meatballs in sauce** and **gnocchi all'amatriciana**.

## Otello alla Concordia, cinema's post-war hangout

**Otello alla Concordia** is a trattoria in a palace courtyard near Piazza di Spagna.
According to the entries for the documentary *Il segreto di Otello* (2015, Filmitalia and
Cineuropa), right after the Second World War young directors and screenwriters got into
the habit of meeting here, among them **Fellini**, **Scola** and **Sordi**: producers
phoned the restaurant directly to reach them, and at those tables pages of neorealism and
Italian comedy were born. The same sources say the Wednesday-evening gatherings still go on.

**Address:** Turismo Roma gives **40/a Via Mario de' Fiori**
