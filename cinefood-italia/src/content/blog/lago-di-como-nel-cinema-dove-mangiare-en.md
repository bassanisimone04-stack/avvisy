Few places have a film career like **Lake Como**. Its villas, gardens and
lakeside towns have appeared in Hollywood blockbusters and Italian films. Here are
the most famous locations and where to eat with a view of the water.

## A lake made for the big screen

- **Star Wars – Attack of the Clones**: the **Villa del Balbianello** in Lenno is
  the planet Naboo, backdrop to Anakin and Padmé's wedding;
- **Casino Royale (007)**: again Villa del Balbianello, where Bond recovers;
- **Ocean's Twelve**: the lake's villas frame Danny Ocean's crew;
- **House of Gucci**: Villa Balbiano hosts the luxurious scenes of the Gucci
  family.

## Where to eat on Lake Como

Lake Como cuisine is refined and tied to the lake. Try:

- **Risotto with perch**, the signature dish;
- **Missoltini** (dried shad) with polenta;
- **Pizzoccheri** from nearby Valtellina;
- The great red wines of Valtellina.

Many restaurants overlook the water directly, for a truly cinematic dinner.

## An itinerary between villas and villages

Bellagio, Varenna, Lenno and Como are the classic stops, between ferries, gardens
and historic residences. A weekend as elegant as the stars who chose it.

## In short

Lake Como is an open-air set where the luxury of cinema meets the flavours of the
lake. Discover the other **[films](/en/film)** and their food locations.
