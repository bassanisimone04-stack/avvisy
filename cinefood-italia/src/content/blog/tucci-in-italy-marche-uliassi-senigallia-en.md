Season two of ***Tucci in Italy***, the National Geographic series released in May 2026,
devotes its third episode to **Le Marche**. It's a different series from *Searching for
Italy*, which was CNN's: the host is still Stanley Tucci, but the production is another.
The restaurant we can point to with a verified address is **Uliassi**.

## Uliassi, in Senigallia

**[Uliassi](/en/locale/uliassi-senigallia)** is at **Banchina di Levante 6**, in Senigallia. It is a
**three-Michelin-star** restaurant (Guide Italia 2026), the only three-star in Le Marche,
run by siblings **Mauro** (kitchen) and **Catia** (front of house) Uliassi. It began in
**1990** as a family-run place on the beach and, after three renovations over twenty
years, reached its present level.

Local press (Corriere Adriatico, Centropagina) reported Tucci's visit to the restaurant,
which took place about two years before the season aired.

## What we haven't listed

In the episode Tucci also visits Campofilone, home of **maccheroncini di Campofilone
IGP**, at the Spinosi family workshop, where pasta is made with flour and eggs as it has
been for generations. It is an artisan workshop, not a restaurant, so we haven't listed it.
