Few TV series have done for Sicilian tourism what *Inspector Montalbano* has.
Fans of Andrea Camilleri's character sooner or later set off for the Ragusa area
to look for two things: **the filming locations** and, above all, **the food** —
because Montalbano famously eats the dishes of tradition in reverent silence.
Here is how to retrace his tour of flavours.

## Where Inspector Montalbano ate

The signature restaurant is
**[Enzo a Mare](/en/locale/enzo-a-mare-punta-secca)**, on the seafront of
**[Punta Secca](/en/punta-secca)**, a short walk from the inspector's famous
house. This is where, on screen, Montalbano sits at his favourite table and
orders the catch of the day, often with his colleagues or his fiancée Livia.
Ragusa-style seafood cuisine: raw fish, pasta with sardines, fried fish and, of
course, freshly caught fish.

> Practical tip: always check opening hours before you travel — seaside spots
> follow the seasons.

## The legendary dishes of the series

The real culinary star of Montalbano is humble, generous Sicilian cooking. Not
to be missed:

- **Pasta 'ncasciata**, the baked pasta timbale with aubergine, caciocavallo
  and ragù that appears in several episodes;
- **Arancini** (with ragù or butter), to which Camilleri even dedicated a short
  story, *Montalbano's Arancini*;
- **Caponata** and seafood starters, the inspector's aperitivo ritual;
- The **fresh fish** of the Sicilian Channel, cooked simply.

## An itinerary through the filming locations

Punta Secca is the heart, but the fictional "Vigàta" is a mosaic of Ragusa-area
towns: **Scicli** (the police station and town hall), **Ragusa Ibla** with its
Baroque backdrops, **Modica** and the beaches of **Sampieri**. A weekend is
enough to combine the sea, UNESCO Baroque and great food.

To plan your stops, start from the
**[Inspector Montalbano](/en/film/il-commissario-montalbano)** page and see all
the connected venues on the **[map](/en/mappa)**.

## In short

Retracing Montalbano's itinerary means eating like he does: sitting by the sea,
with a plate of fish and the right slowness. Punta Secca and
**[Enzo a Mare](/en/locale/enzo-a-mare-punta-secca)** are the perfect starting
point for a journey between cinema and the flavours of Sicily. Heading north towards
Messina, add **[The Godfather in Savoca and Forza d'Agrò](/en/blog/padrino-location-savoca-forza-dagro-sicilia)** to the trip.
