The **Puglia** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* covers the heel of
Italy and, for a stretch, neighbouring Basilicata. These are the three venues
from the episode that we can point to with a verified address, according to CNN's
guide to the second season.

## Antichi Sapori, in Montegrosso

In **Montegrosso**, a hamlet of Andria, **[Antichi Sapori](/en/locale/antichi-sapori-montegrosso)**
is **Pietro Zito**'s restaurant, at Piazza Sant'Isidoro 10. The cooking starts from
wild herbs, cheeses, grains and vegetables from the garden. The dish CNN mentions is
burnt-grain orecchiette with fava cream, burrata and black olives. It holds a Bib
Gourmand in the Michelin Guide Italia 2026 and, according to sources, opened in 1993.

## Bere Vecchie, in Cisternino

In the alleyways of **Cisternino**, in the Itria Valley, **[Trattoria Bere
Vecchie](/en/locale/trattoria-bere-vecchie-cisternino)** (Via Regina Elena 8) is a
butcher shop that also cooks on the spot: spit-roasted meat, run by young **Vito Zurlo**.
Reviews recommend booking.

## Vitantonio Lombardo, in Matera

In **Matera**'s Sassi, **[Vitantonio Lombardo](/en/locale/vitantonio-lombardo-matera)**
(Via Madonna delle Virtù 13) has turned an abandoned cave into a one-Michelin-star
restaurant. CNN's article cites dishes such as "I dropped the Egg in the Garden" and
"Drone View of the Murgia". Tasting menus are 5, 7 or 10 courses.

## What we haven't listed

CNN also mentions Bari: the pizza and bread of Panificio Santa Rita and the city's raw
seafood. These are bread and raw-fish stops without a restaurant address we can
verify, so they stay out for now.
