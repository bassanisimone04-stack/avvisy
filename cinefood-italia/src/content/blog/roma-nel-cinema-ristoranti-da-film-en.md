No city has been on the big screen quite like **Rome**. Among historic trattorias,
elegant cafés and dreamlike terraces, the capital is an open-air set. Here are
**five Roman venues** linked to cinema, with the film that made them famous and what
to order.

## 1. La Veranda dell'Hotel Columbus — *The Great Beauty*
In the frescoed hall of Palazzo della Rovere, Jep Gambardella dines in
Sorrentino's Oscar-winning film. Pure elegance, a stone's throw from St Peter's.
→ **[La Veranda dell'Hotel Columbus](/en/locale/la-veranda-hotel-columbus)**

## 2. Trattoria Santa Lucia — *Eat Pray Love*
Near Piazza Navona, it is one of the Roman locations where Julia Roberts
rediscovers the pleasure of Italian food.
→ **[Trattoria Santa Lucia](/en/locale/trattoria-santa-lucia)**

## 3. Da Pancrazio — *Eat Pray Love*
A historic restaurant built over the remains of the **Theatre of Pompey**,
from the 1st century BC — the very theatre where, according to historical
tradition, Julius Caesar was killed: the lower rooms, among Roman arches
and columns, are an experience literally inside ancient history.
→ **[Da Pancrazio](/en/locale/da-pancrazio)**

## 4. Bar Navona
Among Bernini's fountains, the perfect café for a break in the capital's Baroque
living room, often a backdrop of cinema filmed in Rome.
→ **[Bar Navona](/en/locale/caffe-di-piazza-navona)**

## 5. Café de Paris — *La Dolce Vita*
On Via Veneto, a symbol of Fellini's high-society Rome (now closed, but a piece of
cultural history).
→ **[Café de Paris](/en/locale/cafe-de-paris-roma)**
