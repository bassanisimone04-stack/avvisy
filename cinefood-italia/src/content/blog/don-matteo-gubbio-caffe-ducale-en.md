For thirteen seasons, *Don Matteo* made **Gubbio** its fictional Umbria:
the longest-running Italian crime-comedy series used the Umbrian town as
its fixed set from 2000 to 2011, before moving to Spoleto. And one café,
more than any other place, has stayed tied to those years.

## Piazza Grande, the heart of filming

**Piazza Grande**, Gubbio's scenic main square overlooking the Umbrian
valley, was the series' recurring backdrop for eight seasons. Under its
arcades sits **[Caffè Ducale](/en/locale/caffe-ducale-gubbio)**, the café
that became as familiar to viewers as the leads themselves.

## Cecchini and Don Matteo's chess games

This is where **Sergeant Cecchini** (Nino Frassica) and **Don Matteo**
(Terence Hill) play their daily chess games, one of the show's most
beloved recurring scenes. The venue, still open to the public today,
keeps several props left over from filming — an informal little museum
for anyone visiting Gubbio on the trail of the show.

## Gubbio beyond Don Matteo

The Umbrian town is worth a visit in its own right: the **Palazzo dei
Consoli**, the cable car up to **Monte Ingino**, and one of Umbria's
best-preserved medieval old towns. Anyone following in Don Matteo's
footsteps can easily pair a walk through these sights with a stop at
Caffè Ducale.
