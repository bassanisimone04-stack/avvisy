Twenty years after winning over audiences, *The Devil Wears Prada*
returns with a sequel that puts Andy Sachs face to face with Miranda
Priestly again — this time as a media executive, not an assistant. And
Italy is back too: the summer 2025 shoot brought the cast into the heart
of Milan's fashion world, with a tourism effect still being measured
today.

## Palazzo Parigi, the cast's base

For several weeks, **[Caffè Parigi](/en/locale/caffe-parigi-milano)**,
the café-bistrot of the Palazzo Parigi Hotel & Grand Spa between Brera
and the Fashion Quadrilateral, became the production's Milan base.
Scenes were shot in the hotel lobby, at Caffè Parigi, and in the
building's exterior spaces — right during Milan Fashion Week, the
perfect backdrop for a film built around fashion.

## A measurable tourism boom

This isn't just a detail for film buffs: according to **Confcommercio
Milano** data (Cities Analytics), reported by **Variety** and **Il Sole
24 Ore** in August 2026, the film's locations saw an **18%** rise in
international visitors in summer 2026, peaking at **28%** around Palazzo
Clerici. A rare case where a film's tourism impact can be measured in
precise numbers, months after release.

## Breakfast or aperitivo at Caffè Parigi

Caffè Parigi is open to the public every day, with breakfast, light
lunches and evening aperitivo in an elegant setting — the same one the
cast spent weeks in during filming. It's a convenient stop for anyone
visiting Brera or the Fashion Quadrilateral, minutes from the city's
most famous shopping streets.

*The Devil Wears Prada 2* put international attention back on Milan as
