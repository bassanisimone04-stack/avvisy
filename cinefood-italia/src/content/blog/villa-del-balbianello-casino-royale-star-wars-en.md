**Villa del Balbianello** is the Lake Como villa the movies have used more than once:
**Casino Royale**, **Star Wars** and **A Month by the Lake**. It's in **Lenno** (province
of Como), on the tip of the Lavedo promontory, and is run by **FAI**. Here is what was
shot, how to visit and how not to confuse it with **Villa Balbiano**, which is a
different villa.

## Villa del Balbianello and Villa Balbiano: not the same villa

It's the most frequent mistake, even in foreign articles, because of the similar name.
**Villa del Balbianello** (Lenno, FAI, open to visitors) hosts the scenes of *Casino
Royale* and *Star Wars*. **Villa Balbiano** (Ossuccio, private) is the one in *House of
Gucci* and, according to some sources, in *The Devil Wears Prada 2*: the story is in
*[House of Gucci in Milan and on the lake](/en/blog/house-of-gucci-milano)* and in
*[The Devil Wears Prada 2: Filming Locations in Italy](/en/blog/il-diavolo-veste-prada-2-dove-e-stato-girato)*.

## The villa's history and Cardinal Durini's loggia

The villa was built by Cardinal **Angelo Maria Durini** in 1787 (some sources speak of
a purchase in 1785) on the site of a 13th-century Franciscan monastery, at the most
beautiful point of the promontory. The **loggia** that bears his name is considered its
most famous feature, for its open views over the lake.

After Durini it passed to the Porro Lambertenghi and then the Arconati Visconti, who
hosted writers and intellectuals here. In 1919 the American **Butler Ames** bought it;
in 1974 it passed to the explorer **Guido Monzino**, who left it to **FAI** (the bequest
is dated 1988 by the sources).

## The Casino Royale scenes

In *Casino Royale* (2006) the villa is the private clinic where **James Bond** recovers
after the torture. Some sources also recall the scene in which Mathis is taken away by
MI6 agents. For Italy's other Bond locations read
*[James Bond in Italy: where to eat](/en/blog/james-bond-007-italia-dove-mangiare)*.

## The Star Wars scenes: Attack of the Clones

George Lucas chose Villa del Balbianello for *Star Wars: Episode II* (2002), for the
romantic scenes between **Anakin and Padmé** on the planet Naboo. According to one
source that choice brought many other international productions to the lake.

## A Month by the Lake (1995)

In John Irvin's film the villa becomes a **hotel**, in a story set before the Second
World War.

## How to visit Villa del Balbianello

- **Booking:** compulsory for the garden and guided tours (booking.balbianello@fondoambiente.it,
  tel. +39 0344 56110).
- **Tickets (FAI page consulted, to be rechecked):** garden only €15 full, €11 reduced
  (ages 6–18), free for FAI members. Villa and garden with a guided tour of about 50
  minutes: €25 full, €17 reduced, €5 FAI members.
- **Hours:** (to be verified: sources disagree; check fondoambiente.it).
- **On foot:** from Lenno about 1 km, 25 minutes, with a steep stretch.
- **By boat:** paid taxi boat from the Lido di Lenno; there are also Bellagio–Lenno ferries,
  with about 30 minutes' walk from the landing. Check times and fares.
- **By bus:** line C10 from Como to Lenno. Parking at Lenno's Mercato, via degli Artigiani.
