*Hotel Costiera*, the Prime Video original series starring **Jesse
Williams**, released September 24, 2025, chose the Amalfi Coast as the
setting for a story of luxury and mystery. And like *The White Lotus*, the
hotel in the title isn't a built set: it's a real five-star hotel, still
open to guests today.

## Villa Treville, the real Hotel Costiera

The show's fictional hotel is **Villa Treville**, a clifftop luxury
property in **Positano**, a short distance from the town centre. The
villa has a fascinating past: for years it was the private residence of
director **Franco Zeffirelli**, whose artistic legacy still lingers among
the gardens and spaces of the property, today transformed into one of the
coast's most exclusive hotels.

## Maestro's, the villa's real restaurant

Unlike much of fiction, the food here is verifiable too: the villa's
gourmet restaurant is called **Maestro's**, led by chef **Vincenzo
Castaldo** with input from Michelin-starred chef **Antonio Mellino**. The
cuisine is **km0**, with ingredients grown in the property's own gardens,
served on the restaurant's panoramic terrace, in the legendary Bianca
Salon with the Bianca Bar, or at the new Botanical Bar.

## The plot and setting

In the show, Jesse Williams plays Daniel De Luca, a former Marine of
Italian descent working as a "problem solver" at one of the world's most
luxurious hotels, on the trail of the owner's missing daughter. Six
episodes, all released together on launch day.

## What to eat on the Amalfi Coast

Beyond Maestro's, Positano and its surroundings are home to cuisine that
smells of lemons: **scialatielli with seafood**, **lemon delizia**,
**sfusato amalfitano** lemons, and fresh Tyrrhenian fish — exactly the
flavours also celebrated in our guide on
**[the Amalfi Coast in Ripley](/en/blog/ripley-netflix-atrani-costiera-amalfitana)**.

## In short

*Hotel Costiera* is another example of how fiction, on the Amalfi Coast,
often overlaps with real, bookable places: Villa Treville and its
