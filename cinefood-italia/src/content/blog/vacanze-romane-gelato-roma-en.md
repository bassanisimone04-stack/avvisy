*Roman Holiday* (1953), directed by **William Wyler**, made **Rome**
eternal in the imagination of the whole world. Princess Ann, played by
**Audrey Hepburn** opposite **Gregory Peck**, tours the city on a Vespa,
cuts her hair and tastes freedom… with a gelato in hand. A production
detail that wasn't a given at the time: it was the first major Hollywood
film shot **entirely on location**, between real Rome and Cinecittà
studios, in an era when major studios preferred rebuilding everything back
in Los Angeles. Here is the itinerary through the film's places and the
flavours to try.

## Where Roman Holiday was filmed: the gelato on the Spanish Steps

The gelato scene on the **Spanish Steps** is one of the most famous in film
history. Even today, an artisan gelato while strolling through the centre is the
sweetest way to experience Rome like Audrey.

## The (partly improvised) Mouth of Truth scene

One of the film's most beloved sequences takes place at the **Mouth of
Truth**, inside the Church of Santa Maria in Cosmedin: Gregory Peck puts
his hand into the stone mouth and pretends it has been bitten off, hiding
it inside his jacket. A detail few people know: it **wasn't in the
script**. It was the actor's own improvisation to scare young Audrey
Hepburn, who hadn't been warned — her startled reaction and scream in the
scene are therefore completely genuine.

## The film's other locations

The itinerary also takes in **Palazzo Barberini**, **Via delle Quattro
Fontane**, the **Colosseum**, **Castel Sant'Angelo** and the squares of the
historic centre. A perfect tour of timeless monuments and views.

## What to taste in the Rome of the film

Beyond gelato, the Rome of *Roman Holiday* is one of historic cafés and
trattorias: treat yourself to a **cappuccino** at the bar, a **maritozzo** with
cream, and in the evening authentic Roman cuisine, from cacio e pepe to
carciofi alla giudia. Rome is the city with the most food locations on our site:
discover them on the **[Rome](/en/roma)** page.

## In short

*Roman Holiday* is an invitation to experience Rome with lightness: a
Vespa, a staircase and a gelato, in a city the film captured for real,
street by street. A classic that, decades later, still makes you want to
travel. Browse the other **[films](/en/film)** set in the capital.
