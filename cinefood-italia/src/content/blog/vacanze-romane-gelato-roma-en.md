*Roman Holiday* (1953) made **Rome** eternal in the imagination of the whole
world. Princess Ann, played by **Audrey Hepburn**, tours the city on a Vespa, cuts
her hair and tastes freedom… with a gelato in hand. Here is the itinerary through
the film's places and the flavours to try.

## The gelato on the Spanish Steps

The gelato scene on the **Spanish Steps** is one of the most famous in film
history. Even today, an artisan gelato while strolling through the centre is the
sweetest way to experience Rome like Audrey.

## The film's other locations

The itinerary takes in the **Mouth of Truth**, the **Colosseum**, **Castel
Sant'Angelo** and the squares of the historic centre. A perfect tour of timeless
monuments and views.

## What to taste in the Rome of the film

Beyond gelato, the Rome of *Roman Holiday* is one of historic cafés and
trattorias: treat yourself to a **cappuccino** at the bar, a **maritozzo** with
cream, and in the evening authentic Roman cuisine. Rome is the city with the most
food locations on our site: discover them on the **[Rome](/en/roma)** page.

## In short

*Roman Holiday* is an invitation to experience Rome with lightness: a Vespa, a
staircase and a gelato. A classic that, decades later, still makes you want to
travel. Browse the other **[films](/en/film)** set in the capital.
