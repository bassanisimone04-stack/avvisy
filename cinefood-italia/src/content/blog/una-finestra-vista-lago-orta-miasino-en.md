The Rai 1 series ***Una finestra vista lago***, based on **Andrea Vitali**'s novels and
directed by **Marco Pontecorvo**, is set in **Bellamo**, a village on Lake Como. Bellamo,
though, does not exist: it was built somewhere else, a couple of lakes to the west.

## The real set: Lake Orta

Filming centred on **Lake Orta**, in the province of Novara, between **Orta San Giulio**
and **Miasino**. Film Commission Torino Piemonte followed the shoot in Miasino in spring
2025, and ANSA reported from the set.

In **Orta San Giulio** you can recognise:

- the area around **Piazza Motta**, the village's drawing room on the water;
- the **Broletto**, the 15th-century town hall at the centre of the square;
- the climb to the church of **Santa Maria Assunta**;
- the **little harbour** where boats leave for the **island of San Giulio**.

In **Miasino**, a few kilometres above the lake, the old centre continues the Bellamo of
the series: streets and facades carry on naturally from the scenes shot by the water. Of
Lake Como, what mainly remains in the series is the **Cernobbio jetty**.

## Where to eat on Lake Orta

On the **island of San Giulio**, a few minutes by boat from Piazza Motta, is
**[Ristorante San Giulio](/en/locale/ristorante-san-giulio-orta)**, set in an 18th-century
villa. **It is not a location of this series**: we mention it because it is in the same
village and because it is already in our directory as a location of another film,
Giuseppe Tornatore's *[The Correspondence](/en/film/la-corrispondenza)*, for which it was
dressed in Portuguese style.

For now, no restaurant or bar is listed among the sets of *Una finestra vista lago*: if we
find a documented one, we will add it.

## Two lakes, two series

Lake Orta and Lake Maggiore are half an hour apart by car, and in 2026 they hosted two
different productions. If you are planning a trip, also read
*[The Gentlemen 2 on Lake Maggiore](/en/blog/the-gentlemen-2-lago-maggiore-verbania)*. The
rest of the Italian sets are in the **[films](/en/film)** section and on the
**[map](/en/mappa)**.
