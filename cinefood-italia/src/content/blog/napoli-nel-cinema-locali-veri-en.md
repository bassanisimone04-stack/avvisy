Naples is one of international cinema and television's most loved
cities: its alleys, squares and historic venues have appeared in very
different productions, from Hollywood to Rai fiction. Here are three
real addresses where the big and small screen met Neapolitan food
culture.

## L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele — Eat Pray Love

In the heart of Forcella, **[L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele](/en/locale/pizzeria-da-michele-napoli)**,
open since 1870, is the pizzeria where Liz (Julia Roberts) enjoys the
famous margherita in *[Eat Pray Love](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)*
(2010). A menu stripped to the essentials — marinara and margherita —
and the queue outside that's now part of the experience.

## Gran Caffè Gambrinus — Commissioner Ricciardi

Founded in 1860 and facing Piazza del Plebiscito,
**[Gran Caffè Gambrinus](/en/locale/gran-caffe-gambrinus-napoli)** was
transformed into the 1930s café frequented by the protagonist of
*[Commissioner Ricciardi](/en/blog/commissario-ricciardi-napoli-gambrinus)*.
After the show's success, the table used on set was permanently
reserved, with a dedicated plaque.

## ScottoJonno — My Brilliant Friend

In the elegant Galleria Principe di Napoli, facing the National
Archaeological Museum, **[ScottoJonno](/en/locale/scottojonno-napoli)**
is a café-bistro born as a 19th-century café-chantant and reopened in
2023, featured in the fourth season of
*[My Brilliant Friend](/en/blog/amica-geniale-napoli-scottojonno)*.

## In short

Three different eras — 1870 at Da Michele, 1860 at Gambrinus, 2023 at
