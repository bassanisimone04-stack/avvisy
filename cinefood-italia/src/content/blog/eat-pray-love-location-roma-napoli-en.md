*Eat Pray Love* (2010), directed by **Ryan Murphy** with **Julia Roberts**,
spends its Italian chapter on food, friends and the pleasure of doing
nothing. If you want to retrace it, the good news is that Italy's part of the
film is compact: **Rome** first, then a short stop in **Naples**. This guide
lists the places that film-location portals and regional tourism sites
associate with the film, and separates what is confirmed from what is only
reported by food press.

## Rome: where Liz lives, walks and eats

The centre of Rome is the film's setting for the chapter on food. According to
the location listings, Liz stays on **Via dei Portoghesi**, near Via della
Scrofa: the façade of her apartment is **Palazzo Scapucci**, also known as
Palazzo della Scimmia. She walks alone along **Via del Corso** and through
**Piazza Navona**, where she eats gelato on a bench in front of the church of
**Sant'Agnese in Agone**. Other places tied to the film are **Piazza di
Spagna**, **Via Condotti** and **Campo de' Fiori**.

Regione Lazio's tourism site adds **Villa Borghese** and the alleys of
**Trastevere**, where Liz spends a day with new friends.

## The Roman trattorias: what we can and can't confirm

The food scenes are the film's best-known part, but the restaurants are a
grey area. Food and culture press point to three Roman venues: the
**[Trattoria Santa Lucia](/en/locale/trattoria-santa-lucia)** in Largo Febo,
**Vecchio Orso** on Via dei Gigli d'Oro and
**[Da Pancrazio](/en/locale/da-pancrazio)**, built over the remains of the
Theatre of Pompey. We list the first and the last on the site, but the link
comes from editorial sources rather than from an institutional film portal, so
we suggest treating them as "reported" rather than officially confirmed. Read
more in our guide to [Roman trattorias in the movies](/en/blog/roma-a-tavola-nel-cinema).

## Naples: Via dei Tribunali and the pizza

The Naples segment opens with a walk along **Via dei Tribunali**, in the
Forcella district, with Liz's friend Sofi. Then comes the scene that made the
film famous among food lovers: the pizza at
**[L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele](/en/locale/pizzeria-da-michele-napoli)**, on
Via Cesare Sersale, near Corso Umberto I. This one is confirmed by the Italian
Ministry of Culture's film-location portal. Everything you need is in our
article on the [Eat Pray Love pizza place](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli).

## A simple two-day itinerary

- **Day 1, Rome**: start from Piazza Navona and Sant'Agnese in Agone, then
  walk to Campo de' Fiori and Piazza di Spagna. Lunch in a Roman trattoria.
- **Day 2, Naples**: walk Via dei Tribunali in the morning and queue for a
  margherita at Da Michele, which does not take bookings.

## What to eat, film by film

In Rome, order the classics of the film's food chapter: **cacio e pepe**,
**carbonara**, **carciofi alla giudia**. In Naples, the order is short:
**marinara** or **margherita**. See all Roman venues on our
[Rome page](/en/roma) and all Neapolitan ones on the [Naples page](/en/napoli),
or open the [Eat Pray Love film page](/en/film/mangia-prega-ama).
