**James Bond in Matera** is one of the most photographed Italian sets of recent years:
to shoot *No Time to Die* (2021) the crew worked for about a month and a half in the
Sassi, in the summer of 2019, with Daniel Craig and Léa Seydoux. But the film mixes up
its places: the famous bridge isn't in Matera, the hotel doesn't exist and the motorbike
jump took thousands of litres of Coca-Cola. Here are the real locations, a walking
route and a place to sit down for a meal.

## James Bond in Matera locations: the map at a glance

There is no single official map, and guides disagree on some details: this is a summary
of the places that appear in the soundest sources.

- **Sasso Barisano**: the arrival tunnel, rebuilt for the film near the church of
  Sant'Agostino (Italy for Movies).
- **Via Madonna delle Virtù**: the street of the Aston Martin chase.
- **Piazza Vittorio Veneto and Via XX Settembre**: shot on 15 September 2019 with
  Craig and Seydoux, according to local reports.
- **Piazza Duomo and Via Gradoni Duomo**: the motorbike jump, according to guides.
- **Piazzetta Pascoli**: where the hotel room overlooking the Sassi was built.
- **Madonna delle Vergini**: the cemetery, rebuilt for the film.
- **Gravina in Puglia**: the bridge, about thirty kilometres away.

## The James Bond scenes in Matera, one by one

In the Sassi you can find five moments of the film, and it helps to spot them one by one:

- **The arrival in town**: the tunnel exit and the stepping out of the car, in the Sasso Barisano.
- **The hotel room**: the balcony overlooking the Sassi, built on purpose.
- **The chase**: the Aston Martin DB5 among stairways and squares.
- **The motorbike jump**: the most acrobatic scene, around the Cathedral.
- **The cemetery and the bridge**: rebuilt near Madonna delle Vergini and in Gravina.

In 2019 Matera was European Capital of Culture: the film brought a second wave of
visitors to the Sassi, and several agencies now run dedicated tours.

## The James Bond bridge scene: Matera in the film, Gravina in real life

If you are looking for the *James Bond bridge in Matera*, here is the inconvenient part:
it isn't in Matera. The bridge and cemetery scene was shot on the **Ponte Acquedotto
Madonna della Stella** in **Gravina in Puglia**, in the province of Bari, while the
Sassi backdrop was added digitally. To protect it during filming, in September 2019,
the bridge was lined with steel sheets.

The bridge is far older than the film: sources date it to the 17th century, it collapsed
in the 1722 earthquake and was rebuilt in the mid-18th century as an aqueduct, at the
Orsini family's behest. It is worth a half-day detour. In September 2026 it also made
the news: a 110-seat table was set up on it, in the American "Outstanding in the Field"
format, at 390 dollars a head, sparking a debate about using a public monument.

## Chase and motorbike jump: the James Bond Matera chase scene

The Aston Martin DB5 chase runs through the narrow lanes and stairways of the Sasso
Barisano, with **Via Madonna delle Virtù** among the streets named by local reports.
Then comes the jump: Bond rides a motorbike up a stone staircase, clears a wall and
lands in the square in front of the Cathedral. Guides place it between **Via Gradoni
Duomo** and **Piazza Duomo**.

And the **Coca-Cola**? To make the paving grippier, the production poured it on the
street. According to stunt coordinator Lee Morrison, quoted by Total Film, about 8,400
gallons were used, around 30,000 litres, at a cost of nearly 60,000 euros. The Italian
press credits the idea to Daniel Craig.

## Does the James Bond hotel in Matera exist?

No. The room with the balcony over the Sassi where Bond and Madeleine stay is a **set**:
Italy for Movies says it was built near **Piazzetta Pascoli** and then dismantled.
Several guides say the film's hotel, called Palazzo Lucareschi, is invented. Online
you can read where the crew slept, but sources contradict each other, so we don't state
it as fact.

For the same view, go to the **Belvedere in Piazza Pascoli**: it is free and the framing
is the one from the film.

## The James Bond Matera route: a walking map

A composite route, 3 to 4 hours on foot with the stairways:

1. **Piazza Vittorio Veneto**, for the overview.
2. **Piazza Duomo**, for the motorbike jump.
3. **Sasso Barisano**, heading down towards the church of Sant'Agostino.
4. **Via Madonna delle Virtù**, for the chase.
5. **Belvedere in Piazza Pascoli**, for the hotel room.
6. By car: the viewpoint on Madonna delle Vergini and the bridge in Gravina.

## Where to eat in Matera in 007's footsteps

For lunch there is an address right on the chase street:
**[Vitantonio Lombardo](/en/locale/vitantonio-lombardo-matera)**, at **Via Madonna delle
Virtù 13**, a one-Michelin-star restaurant in a restored cave, with tasting menus of five,
seven or ten courses. It is not a location in the film: we list it because it is on the
same street and because it appears in the Puglia episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching
for Italy*, covered in *[Tucci in Puglia: Antichi Sapori and Matera](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-puglia-antichi-sapori-matera)*.

For a simpler meal, look for Lucanian cooking in the Sassi: **Matera IGP bread**,
**peperoni cruschi**, **cavatelli** and **caciocavallo podolico**.

## Where to sleep in 007's footsteps

- **B&B Sul Ponte**, in **Gravina in Puglia**: according to bondlocations.com, Daniel Craig
  stayed here during the shoot. It's a few steps from the Madonna della Stella aqueduct
  bridge, the film's real bridge. Single source, no independent confirmation;

  Sassi, this is the address to look for ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=B%26B+Sul+Ponte+Gravina+in+Puglia)).
- **Sextantio Le Grotte della Civita**, **28 Via Civita**, Sasso Barisano (address from the
  official site): a hotel in ancient caves. Not a film location; it's in the same area of
  the Sassi and also offers candle-lit dinners in a 13th-century rock-hewn church
  ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sextantio+Le+Grotte+della+Civita+Matera)).
- **Palazzo Gattini**, **13 Piazza Duomo**, Matera (address from several listings): a hotel in
  the old town, above the Sassi. Not a film location ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palazzo+Gattini+Matera)).

## By the sea: Riva dei Ginepri

For a beach day, **Riva dei Ginepri** is a beach club with a restaurant on the shore of
**Marina di Pisticci** (Wanderlog places it on Via Ulisse). Reviews are mixed: some praise
food and service, others flag cleanliness and quality. Not a film location. [TO VERIFY: hours,
season and booking] ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Riva+dei+Ginepri+Marina+di+Pisticci)).

## Beyond Matera: Maratea and Sapri

For the two leads' trip towards Matera, Italy for Movies lists two more places in the
film: a stretch of the **SS18 coast road near Maratea**, in Basilicata on the Tyrrhenian
sea, and the **railway station of Sapri**, in the province of Salerno. They aren't in the
Sassi, but they complete the map of *No Time to Die*'s Italian locations.
