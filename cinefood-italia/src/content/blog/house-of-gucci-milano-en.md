Ridley Scott's *House of Gucci* tells the rise and fall of the famous fashion
family, with Italy as its elegant backdrop: **Milan**, Lake Como, Rome and
Florence. The fashion capital par excellence takes centre stage. Here is an
itinerary through the Milan of the film and where to eat.

## The Milan of the film

The film breathes Milanese elegance: the **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II**, the
**Duomo**, the historic palaces and the Liberty-style villas (like Villa Necchi
Campiglio) that tell the story of fashion's luxury. A refined Milan, perfect for
an itinerary of shopping, architecture and good food.

## Where to eat in Milan

For an elegant dinner in the heart of the city, the
**[Ristorante Brera](/en/locale/ristorante-brera-milano)**, in the artists'
district of the same name, combines atmosphere and careful cuisine. Discover all
the venues of **[Milan](/en/milano)** on our site.

## What to taste

Milanese cuisine is a timeless classic: **saffron risotto**, **veal cutlet alla
milanese**, **ossobuco** and the ritual of the aperitivo, a true city
institution. A meal worthy of the film's elegance.

## In short

*House of Gucci* is a journey into Italian elegance, and Milan is its capital.
Treat yourself to a refined dinner at the
**[Ristorante Brera](/en/locale/ristorante-brera-milano)** and experience the city
as in the film.
