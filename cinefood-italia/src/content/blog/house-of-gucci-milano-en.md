Ridley Scott's *House of Gucci* (2021), starring **Lady Gaga** and **Adam
Driver**, tells the rise and fall of the famous fashion family, with Italy
as its elegant backdrop: **Milan**, Lake Como, Rome and Florence. The
fashion capital par excellence takes centre stage. Here is an itinerary
through the Milan of the film and where to eat.

## The Milan of the film

The film breathes Milanese elegance: the **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II**, the
**Duomo**, the historic palaces and the Liberty-style villas that tell the
story of fashion's luxury. A refined Milan, perfect for an itinerary of
shopping, architecture and good food.

## The family villa on Lake Como

The scenes set in the lavish family villa, where **Al Pacino** plays
patriarch Aldo Gucci, were filmed at **Villa Balbiano** on Lake Como: a
historic 16th-century residence (according to the sources we consulted,
bought in 1596 by Tolomeo Gallio), now private property. It is the same
villa that, according to some sources, appears in *The Devil Wears Prada 2*:
read **[where it was filmed](/en/blog/il-diavolo-veste-prada-2-dove-e-stato-girato)**. Filming on the lake also touched
Ossuccio and Tremezzina, while other scenes were shot in Rome, Florence and
even in the Alps, at Gressoney.

## Where to eat in Milan

For an elegant dinner in the heart of the city, the
**[Nabucco](/en/locale/ristorante-brera-milano)**, in the Brera artists'
district, combines atmosphere and careful cuisine since 1970 (it is not a filming location: just an address in the district). Discover all
the venues of **[Milan](/en/milano)** on our site.

## What to taste

Milanese cuisine is a timeless classic: **saffron risotto**, **veal cutlet alla
milanese**, **ossobuco** and the ritual of the aperitivo, a true city
institution. A meal worthy of the film's elegance.

You will find the film page **[House of Gucci](/en/film/house-of-gucci)** and the
venues of **[Milan](/en/milano)**.

## In short

*House of Gucci* is a journey into Italian elegance, and Milan is its capital.
Treat yourself to a refined dinner at the
**[Nabucco](/en/locale/ristorante-brera-milano)** and experience the city
as in the film.
