The **Milan** episode of *Stanley Tucci: Searching for Italy* portrays a city less
touristy than you might expect, where the food is cotoletta, risotto and aperitivo.
Here are the two real restaurants from the episode that we could verify.

## Ratanà: cotoletta and risotto

Tucci's first taste of Milan is at **[Ratanà](/en/locale/ratana-milano)**, chef
**Cesare Battisti**'s restaurant at **Via Gaetano de Castillia 28**. Battisti fries
a **cotoletta alla milanese** and explains the story of **risotto alla milanese**.
The venue serves contemporary takes on Milanese and Lombard cooking.

## Osteria del Treno

After a trip to Lake Como, Tucci and his wife Felicity Blunt return to the city to
dine at **[Osteria del Treno](/en/locale/osteria-del-treno-milano)**, on **Via San
Gregorio 46**. According to CNN's guide, for him it embodies the history and spirit
of Milan like almost nowhere else.

## Other places in the episode

The episode also features **Mag Café**, where Tucci discovers the aperitivo ritual,
and **Tencitt**, a bar where he tries a saffron martini. We haven't verified address
and status for these two, so we don't list them.
