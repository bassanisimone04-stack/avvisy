*Ferrari* (2023) by Michael Mann tells the life of Enzo Ferrari and his
**Modena**, the heart of the Motor Valley. But Modena is not only about motors:
it is one of Italy's gastronomic capitals, a land of flavours known all over the
world. Here is the Modena of the film and what to put on the table.

## The Modena of Enzo Ferrari

The film breathes the air of 1950s Modena: the streets, the workshops, the
passion for racing. The elegant, hard-working Emilian city is the perfect backdrop
for the story of "the Drake".

## The flavours of Modena

Here the cuisine is a heritage. Don't miss:

- **Tortellini in broth**, the queen of the Emilian table;
- Hand-made **tortelloni** and **tagliatelle**;
- **Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena PDO**, aged for decades;
- **Parmigiano Reggiano**, **Lambrusco** and **zampone**.

Modena is also home to world-famous fine dining: a paradise for food lovers.

## An itinerary in the Motor Valley

Beyond the table, the area offers the motor museums (Ferrari, Lamborghini,
Pagani) and the art cities of Emilia. A weekend between speed and slowness at the
table.

## In short

*Ferrari* is a tribute to Modena, where motors and good food run at the same
speed. The perfect destination for those who love cinema and taste. Browse the
other **[films](/en/film)** and their food locations.
