Thirty years after its release, *Honeymoons* (*Viaggi di nozze*) remains
one of **Carlo Verdone**'s best-loved comedies: three stories of
weddings gone wrong, with a cast that made 1990s Italian audiences laugh
— and occasionally tear up.

## Ivano and Jessica's off-key wedding

The first story follows Ivano (Verdone) and Jessica (**Claudia
Gerini**), a couple whose wedding is plagued by misunderstandings from
the ceremony onward. The vows are exchanged at the **Church of San
Giuseppe Cottolengo**, on Viale Valle Aurelia in Rome — a real location,
not a built set.

## The reception at Paradiso Terrestre

After the ceremony, the celebrations move to
**[Ristorante Paradiso Terrestre](/en/locale/ristorante-paradiso-terrestre-roma)**,
on Via delle Capannelle, in southern Rome. It's here that much of the
episode's comedy of errors plays out — in a venue that, thirty years
later, still specialises in exactly this: wedding receptions.

## A thirtieth anniversary worth remembering

In July 2025, marking thirty years since its release, the film returned
to entertainment pages with retrospectives revisiting its locations —
officially confirming the link between the film and the restaurant on
Via delle Capannelle.

## In short

*Honeymoons* proves that even the lightest-looking comedies can hide
real, verifiable locations. Paradiso Terrestre is still there, ready to
host other receptions — hopefully calmer than Ivano and Jessica's.
