Cinema has made many small Italian villages immortal. Visiting them means
combining two pleasures: walking among the film locations and sitting down to
taste the flavours of the territory. Here are eight stops not to miss.

## South and islands

- **Savoca (Sicily)** — the alleys of *The Godfather* and the village bar's granita.
- **Palazzo Adriano (Sicily)** — the square of *Cinema Paradiso*.
- **Punta Secca (Sicily)** — the house and seafood flavours of *Montalbano*.
- **Salina (Aeolian Islands)** — the atmosphere of *Il Postino* among capers and malvasia.
- **Castellabate (Cilento)** — the comedy of *Welcome to the South* and fish on the port.

## Centre and North

- **Rome** — a chapter of its own, from *The Great Beauty* to *Eat Pray Love*.
- **Crema (Lombardy)** — the summer of *Call Me by Your Name*.
- **Venice** — the historic cafés of *The Talented Mr. Ripley*.
