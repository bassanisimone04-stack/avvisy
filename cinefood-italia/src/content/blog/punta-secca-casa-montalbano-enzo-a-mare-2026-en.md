Punta Secca, the "Marinella" of *Inspector Montalbano*, is one of the most
visited destinations for fans of the series. 2026 brought big news: a storm
surge, rebuilding work and new projects in the inspector's house. Here is what
we know, with sources, and what is worth checking before you travel. For the
full tour of food and filming locations, also read
**[Enzo a Mare: Montalbano's Restaurant in Sicily](/en/blog/dove-mangiava-montalbano-sicilia)**.

## Cyclone Harry and Enzo a Mare

Cyclone Harry hit the coasts of Sicily, Sardinia and Calabria from 19 January
2026. In Punta Secca it caused extensive damage to **Enzo a Mare**, the
restaurant of the series, as several outlets reported: *La Sicilia* described
the devastation on 6 February.

The same day the owners answered the alarming news through *Ragusanews*: in
their words the terrace had not disappeared, the damage was limited and partly
repaired already, and rebuilding work was under way ahead of summer.

We found no more recent official statement on the reopening. An *Everyeye*
article of 4 November 2026 describes it as the restaurant on Lungomare Amerigo
Vespucci, without saying whether or when it is operating again. **Before you
plan a lunch here, check the venue's official channels** or call to confirm
that it is open and its hours.

## Montalbano's house: B&B, restaurant and pizzeria

Montalbano's house is at Corso Aldo Moro 44, a few steps from the lighthouse
and Torre Scalambri, and it is a bed & breakfast. In its own listing the B&B
announced a closure from November 2025 to March 2026, with bookings for the
2026 season (April to November) opening on 29 December.

In late December 2025 the owner told *Repubblica* that he had rented the house
to chef **Joseph Micieli** for a fine-dining restaurant with a sea view on the
ground floor. In August 2026 *La Sicilia* instead reported the opening, on the
house's terrace, of a "seaside" pizzeria with dishes by Adelina. The two
reports describe different projects and we found no confirmation of how they
coexist with the B&B: **check the latest information before you book**.

## Planning your visit

- **Punta Secca** is about 20 km from Ragusa. Its name comes from the rocks
  that surface in front of the beach, the "secca".
- The **lighthouse** and **Torre Scalambri** are within walking distance of the
  house.
- For the series' other locations, **Scicli**, **Ragusa Ibla**, **Modica** and
  the **Fornace Penna** (the "Mannara" of the show) are all nearby.
- The seafront where Montalbano walks is the one in Donnalucata, not the one
  in Punta Secca.

The venue's page is **[Enzo a Mare](/en/locale/enzo-a-mare-punta-secca)**. On
the **[map](/en/mappa)** you will also find the other venues linked to the
inspector.
