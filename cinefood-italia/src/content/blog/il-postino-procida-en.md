**Il Postino in Procida**: if you think Michael Radford's 1994 film was shot only in the
Aeolian Islands, you need a correction. The fishing village we see is **Marina
Corricella**, in **Procida**, while Salina is the backdrop for the poet's house. Here is
what was shot on each island, what you can walk, and where to eat. For the Aeolians
there is the guide to *[Il Postino in Salina](/en/blog/il-postino-salina-eolie)*.

## Procida or Salina? Where Il Postino was filmed

Sources agree on one point: the film has no single island. **Procida** (Campania) is
the village where Mario and Beatrice live; **Salina**, in the Aeolian Islands, hosts the
scenes at Neruda's house and the bike rides (italia.it); **Pantelleria** is Italy for
Movies' pick for some opening scenes with Mario on his bike. The inn's **interiors** were
rebuilt at **Cinecittà**.

## Procida, the film's natural setting

Procida is small, which helps: in a few hours you can see most of the locations. The
ones sources confirm are:

- **Marina Corricella**: the fishing village with pastel-coloured houses is the heart of
  the film. A small square here is named after **Massimo Troisi**: according to one source
  it was dedicated in 2009.
- **Beatrice's inn**, which still exists today (see below).
- **The church of Madonna delle Grazie**: the procession that ends in the sea starts
  here, according to Italy for Movies.
- **The post office**: Italy for Movies places it in Procida. Exactly where is not
  confirmed, so we won't send you looking for a precise address.

Practical tip: land at **Marina Grande**, where ferries from Naples and Pozzuoli dock,
and reach Corricella on foot or with a short bus or taxi ride. The village takes an
hour to walk; the rest of your time is for lunch.

## La Locanda del Postino in Marina Corricella: the restaurant to visit

It's the address most tied to the film: **La Locanda del Postino** is in Marina Corricella,
at **43 Via Marina di Corricella** (80079 Procida), the address given by italia.it, Suitcase
Magazine and a fan forum. According to these sources it's the inn where Beatrice (Maria
Grazia Cucinotta) works, and in front is the square dedicated to Troisi. It's not
confirmed that filming took place here (the interiors are from Cinecittà): it's a
venue tied to the film. A 2026 event
page gives number 14, though: check before you go.

You come here for **fresh fish and shellfish** served on the harbour. The reviews
collected by Wanderlog are mixed on portions and the price of fish, but praise the red tuna
and the spaghetti with mussels: honest, not perfect, and the view is worth the meal.

**Practical information**

- **Where:** 43 Via Marina di Corricella, 80079 Procida NA ([open the map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Locanda+del+Postino+Procida)).
- **Status:** it appears to be operating; we recommend checking opening and days before

## A half-day itinerary in Mario's footsteps

1. **Land at Marina Grande** and head up to Corricella.
2. **Walk around Marina Corricella**, with the Troisi square and the harbour.
3. **Lunch at La Locanda del Postino**, sea view.
4. **Church of Madonna delle Grazie** and a stroll through the town.
5. For the film's other scenes: **[Salina](/en/blog/il-postino-salina-eolie)** and its
   Pollara, on a separate day.
