A murdered inspector investigating his own killing, suspended between
the living and the dead: *The Red Door* (*La porta rossa*), a Rai 2
noir series in three seasons, found in Mitteleuropean Trieste the
perfect set for its suspended, melancholy mood.

## Trieste, a silent lead

Across three seasons (2017-2023), the series starring **Lino Guanciale**
and **Gabriella Pession** used Trieste not as mere backdrop but as a
genuine co-star: its Liberty-style architecture, its squares facing the
sea and its historic cafés carry a mood as dark as the plot itself.

## Caffè San Marco, a place for decisive meetings

Among the recurring locations is the **[Antico Caffè San
Marco](/en/locale/caffe-san-marco-trieste)**, open since 1914:
according to the official film tourism portal of the Friuli Venezia
Giulia region, this is where the characters of judge **Anna Mayer** and
magistrate **Antonio Piras** meet, in one of the show's key moments.

## One of Trieste's most-used sets

Caffè San Marco is no stranger to film and TV: the same venue also
hosted filming for the Sky series *[Il
Re](/en/blog/il-re-trieste-caffe-san-marco)*, starring Luca Zingaretti,
and for the film *[Diabolik - Ginko
Attacks!](/en/blog/diabolik-ginko-trieste-caffe-san-marco)* by the
Manetti Bros. — proof of how its timeless atmosphere keeps attracting
very different productions.

*The Red Door* confirms Caffè San Marco as one of the most versatile,
