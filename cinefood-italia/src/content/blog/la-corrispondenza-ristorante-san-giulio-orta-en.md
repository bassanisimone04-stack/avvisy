*La corrispondenza* (2016) by **Giuseppe Tornatore**, with **Jeremy Irons** and
**Olga Kurylenko**, has among its locations a restaurant on an island: **[Ristorante
San Giulio](/en/locale/ristorante-san-giulio-orta)**, on the island of San Giulio, on
Lake Orta. So says Italy for Movies, the Ministry of Culture's location portal.

## The restaurant

Ristorante San Giulio is housed in an 18th-century villa at **Via Basilica 4**, and is
reached by boat from the landing at **Piazza Motta**, in Orta San Giulio. It serves
Piedmontese and lake cooking. For the film the venue was temporarily dressed in
**Portuguese style**, according to Italy for Movies.

## What we don't know

The sources we consulted don't describe the scene or say how much of the restaurant
appears on screen: that is why we add nothing more. The film's page is *[La
corrispondenza](/en/film/la-corrispondenza)*.

## What to eat on Lake Orta

Piedmontese and lake cooking: **risotto**, **lake fish** and local **cured meats**. For Piedmont on
television read *[Stanley Tucci in Piedmont](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-piemonte-del-cambio-vercelli)*, or
the guide to northern film places *[Cinema in the North](/en/blog/cinema-al-nord-milano-laghi)*.
