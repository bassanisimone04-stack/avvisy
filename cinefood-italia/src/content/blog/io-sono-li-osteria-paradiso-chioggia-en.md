*Io sono Li* (2011) by **Andrea Segre** is set in **Chioggia**, the "little Venice" of the
southern lagoon. At its heart is an osteria frequented by fishermen, where the
protagonist, Shun Li, works. The **Italy for Movies** portal lists an osteria in
Chioggia among the locations, and secondary sources identify it as
**[Osteria Paradiso](/en/locale/osteria-paradiso-chioggia)**.

## The osteria today

Osteria Paradiso stands along **Fondamenta Canal Lombardo** (number 1281 according to
sources) and is frequented by fishermen. According to sources it displays photos and
posters of the film, a reminder of the shoot. It is open every day from early morning,
with hours to be verified.

## Chioggia, beyond the film

Chioggia is a city of canals and bridges, with its fish market and lagoon cooking
tradition. For more film places in the Veneto read *[Somebody Feed Phil in
Venice](/en/blog/somebody-feed-phil-venezia-ristoranti)*, or the guide to *[Stanley Tucci in
Venice](/en/blog/stanley-tucci-venezia-all-arco-villa-600)*. The film's page is *[Io sono
Li](/en/film/io-sono-li)*.

## What to eat in Chioggia

Lagoon and Adriatic fish: **sarde in saor**, **fritto misto**, **fish risotto**. To drink, an
"ombra" of wine as is the custom in Veneto osterie.
