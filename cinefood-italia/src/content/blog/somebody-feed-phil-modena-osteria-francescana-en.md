The *Somebody Feed Phil* episode devoted to **Venice** (season 2, episode 1) also
includes **Modena**. Phil Rosenthal arrives with guide **Caterina Schenetti** and goes
from Mercato Albinelli to the venues that tell Emilian cooking. Sources are the host's
official website, Phil Rosenthal World, and city guides.

## Osteria Francescana

The main moment is dinner at **[Osteria Francescana](/en/locale/osteria-francescana-modena)**,
**Massimo Bottura**'s three-Michelin-star restaurant at **Via Stella 22**. Phil dines in
the kitchen on a tasting menu that includes five ages of Parmigiano. The same
restaurant also appears in *[Master of None](/en/blog/master-of-none-modena-osteria-francescana)*.

## Bar Tiffany: gnocco fritto

With Caterina, Phil tries **gnocco fritto** at **[Bar
Tiffany](/en/locale/bar-tiffany-modena)**, on **Via Canalino 58**: dough fried in lard,
to eat with cured meats.

## Balsamic vinegar and the market

**La Consorteria 1966**, at Piazza Mazzini 9, is a shop devoted to Traditional Balsamic
Vinegar of Modena PDO: Phil tastes vinegars aged 12 and 25 years. **Mercato Albinelli** is
the city centre's covered market. They are a shop and a market, not venues with tables,
and we mention them without listing them.

## What to eat in Modena, like Phil

**Gnocco fritto**, **tortellini**, **traditional balsamic vinegar** and **Parmigiano
Reggiano**. For the Venice part of the same episode read *[Somebody Feed Phil in
Venice](/en/blog/somebody-feed-phil-venezia-ristoranti)*; for another real Modena venue, *[Hosteria
Giusti](/en/locale/hosteria-giusti-modena)*.
