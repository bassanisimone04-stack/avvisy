Rome has always been a city of cinema, and its **trattorias** have been the
backdrop to scenes etched in our collective memory. Here are three addresses in
the historic centre where the big screen meets good food, all within easy
walking distance of each other.

## Trattoria Santa Lucia — *Eat Pray Love* (2010)

A stone's throw from Piazza Navona, in Largo Febo, this is where **Julia
Roberts** enjoys Roman cuisine in one of the most famous food scenes of *Eat
Pray Love*. A true hymn to the capital's kitchen, in a setting that still
carries the atmosphere seen on screen. In Naples, in the same film, the
actress also treats herself to **[pizza at Da
Michele](/en/blog/eat-pray-love-pizza-da-michele-napoli)**.

## Da Pancrazio — *Eat Pray Love* (2010)

Nearby, in Piazza del Biscione, **Da Pancrazio** is among the film's Roman
locations. The twist? Its lower rooms rise over the remains of the **Theatre of
Pompey**, from the 1st century BC — the very theatre where, according to
historical tradition, Julius Caesar was killed: you dine literally inside
Rome's history.

## La Veranda of Hotel Columbus — *The Great Beauty* (2013)

Beneath the Renaissance frescoes of Palazzo della Rovere, near the Vatican,
**Paolo Sorrentino** set one of the most elegant dinners of his 2014
Oscar-winning film, with Jep Gambardella (Toni Servillo) and Ramona.

> Tip: build your itinerary starting from Piazza Navona and moving towards Campo
> de' Fiori. Always check opening hours and availability with the venues.
