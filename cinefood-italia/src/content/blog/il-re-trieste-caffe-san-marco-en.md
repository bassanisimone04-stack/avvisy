*Il Re* (*The King*), a Sky Original series starring **Luca Zingaretti**,
is set in a fictional border prison. The city framing it, though, is
real: Trieste, with its sea, its Porto Vecchio and its historic cafés.

## Trieste in the series

The **Friuli Venezia Giulia Film Commission**'s film tourism portal
documents the city locations: **Piazza Oberdan**, **Ponterosso**, the
**Porto Vecchio** and the **Antico Caffè San Marco**. Filming took place
in summer 2021 and returned to the city in November 2022 for a second
round, alongside the other sets in Rome, Turin and Civitavecchia.

## Caffè San Marco

**[Caffè San Marco](/en/locale/caffe-san-marco-trieste)** opened in
**1914** on Via Cesare Battisti and quickly became the city's literary
café: the haunt of **Italo Svevo** and **Umberto Saba**, one of the places
where the Mitteleuropean Trieste of the early twentieth century took
shape. The Liberty interiors — wooden masks on the walls, marble tables,
mirrors — are still the originals, and they are what the series brought
on screen: several sequences are shot inside.

Today the San Marco is also a bookshop and a restaurant, open every day
from morning until late. It's one of those places where you can sit for
hours with a book and nobody minds: part of its identity, not a
concession to visitors.

## A day in Trieste following the series

A simple itinerary lines up the series' locations: a morning at **Piazza
Oberdan** and a walk down to **Ponterosso**, lunch or a break at the **San
Marco**, an afternoon in the **Porto Vecchio**, the vast nineteenth-century
port area now being redeveloped. With more time, finish at Piazza Unità
d'Italia and the Molo Audace at sunset.

## In short

*Il Re* used Trieste for what it is: a border city whose architecture
tells the story of more than one empire. Caffè San Marco is the most
