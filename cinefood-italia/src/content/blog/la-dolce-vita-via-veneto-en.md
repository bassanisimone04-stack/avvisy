No street tells the story of cinema like **Via Vittorio Veneto** in Rome. In the
1960s, with Federico Fellini's *La Dolce Vita*, it became the symbol of glamour,
paparazzi and Italian high society.

## The heart of the "dolce vita"

Between elegant hotels and cafés facing the street, Via Veneto was the stage of
the Rome that never slept. Even today, strolling there means breathing that
timeless atmosphere.

## Not only Fellini

Rome has always been a film city: from the trattorias of *Eat Pray Love* to the
terraces of *The Great Beauty*, to the strolls of *To Rome with Love*. Every
corner has its own story on the big screen.
