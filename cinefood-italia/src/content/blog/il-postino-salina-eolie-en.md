*Il Postino* (*The Postman*), Massimo **Troisi**'s final film (1994,
directed by Michael Radford), made the island of **Salina** in the Aeolian
archipelago immortal. Its pebble beaches, dry-stone walls and deep-blue sea
are the backdrop to the friendship between the postman Mario and the poet
Pablo Neruda. The film is loosely based on the novel *Il postino di Neruda*
(*Ardiente paciencia*) by Chilean writer **Antonio Skármeta**. Here is the
itinerary through the locations and the island's flavours.

## A production that made Italian film history

Filming began on March 14, 1994, and lasted 12 weeks, taking place between
Cinecittà, **Salina** and **Procida**. Behind the film's poetic lightness
lies a moving production story: Troisi urgently needed delicate heart
surgery, but chose to finish filming first. He died in his sleep just hours
after shooting wrapped, on the very last night of production — which is why
*Il Postino* is still remembered as his testament film.

## The film's locations in Salina

The heart of filming is **Pollara**, a hamlet of the municipality of Malfa, on
the western side of the island. Pollara beach, sheltered by high rock walls and
facing the Faraglione stack, is where Mario and Neruda have their conversations.
From here, the road climbing to the poet's "red house" offers a view over the
whole archipelago.

## Where to eat in Salina

For a stop at the table, the
**[Trattoria Cucinotta](/en/locale/trattoria-dell-isola-salina)** serves
authentic Aeolian cuisine: fresh fish, Salina capers (among the finest in Italy),
**pane cunzato** and the sweet **Malvasia delle Lipari**. Simple, intense flavours,
just like the film's island.

## What to taste in the Aeolian Islands

Salina's signature products are **capers**, **Malvasia** wine and seafood dishes.
Also try pasta with wild fennel and the humble cuisine of oily fish. A weekend
here is a dive into Mediterranean slowness.

## In short

Salina is *Il Postino* turned into an island: sea, poetry and honest cooking.
Start from the
**[Trattoria Cucinotta](/en/locale/trattoria-dell-isola-salina)** and let the
flavours of the Aeolian Islands win you over.
