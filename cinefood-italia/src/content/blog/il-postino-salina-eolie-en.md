**Il Postino in Salina**: **Massimo Troisi**'s last film (1994, directed by Michael
Radford) made **Pollara**, on the island's west side, famous. The film is loosely based on
*Il postino di Neruda* (*Ardiente paciencia*) by **Antonio Skármeta**. Here is what was
shot in Salina, what has changed since, how to get there and where to eat. For the film's
fishing village read *[Il Postino in Procida](/en/blog/il-postino-procida)*.

## Il Postino: the locations at a glance

- **Pollara** (Salina, municipality of Malfa): the conversations between Mario and
  Neruda, the Faraglione, Mario's house.
- **Procida**: Marina Corricella, the church, the inn (see the dedicated guide).
- **Pantelleria**: some opening scenes with Mario on his bike, according to Italy for Movies.
- **Cinecittà**: the interiors.

Troisi died in June 1994, shortly after filming ended: the film remains his testament.

## Pollara: Mario and Neruda's beach

**Pollara** lies on the western part of the island, in a bay closed in by tall rock walls.
The rock you see from the beach is the **Faraglione**, the backdrop of the dialogues between
Mario and the poet, while to the north Pollara's balate and the natural arch of **Punta
Perciato** complete the landscape. An important note: **the film's beach no longer exists as
it was**. Because of erosion only small emerged areas remain, reachable by swimming or boat.
So go for the view and the atmosphere, not to lie on Mario's pebbles: [TO VERIFY access
conditions before going down].

### And Mario's house?

Sources disagree. According to one, Pollara's **balate** become the home of Mario and his
fisherman father; according to another the «Postman's house» is a house in **contrada
Punta**, Aeolian in style, with a pinkish facade overlooking Pollara's sea (italia.it has a
«Mario's house» page). We don't take sides: if you go, ask in Malfa.

## How to get to Salina

From **Milazzo** Liberty Lines hydrofoils take about 1 hour 35 minutes direct (1 hour 50 with
stops); the Siremar ferry takes about 3 hours 20 and is the only one that carries cars.
The most frequent landing is **Santa Marina Salina**. Times and prices change with the
season: [TO VERIFY on Liberty Lines and Siremar].

## Where to eat in Salina

- **[Trattoria Cucinotta](/en/locale/trattoria-dell-isola-salina)**, **66 Via Risorgimento**,
  Santa Marina Salina: a trattoria in the centre, Aeolian cooking with capers, pane cunzato
  and malvasia. Not a film location ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Trattoria+Cucinotta+Santa+Marina+Salina)).
- **Da Alfredo**, **2 Via Marina Garibaldi**, **Lingua**: the home of Salina's **granita**
  (flavours from almond to mulberry) and **pane cunzato**. Portions are large and at peak
  times you wait; for some reviewers service is slow. Phone 090 984 3075 per Indagare
  [TO VERIFY] ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Da+Alfredo+Lingua+Salina)).
