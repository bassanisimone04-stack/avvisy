**Bar Vitelli in Savoca** is the bar from Francis Ford Coppola's *The Godfather*: it is here that
Michael Corleone, hiding in Sicily, asks her father for permission to meet **Apollonia
Vitelli**. Coppola chose Savoca, a village above Taormina, because the real Corleone was by then
too modern. Here you find the address, hours, what to expect and how to get there.

## Where Bar Vitelli is

**[Bar Vitelli](/en/locale/bar-vitelli-savoca)** is at **7 Piazza Fossia**, at the entrance to
Savoca ([map]({M}Bar+Vitelli+Savoca)), on the ground floor of **Palazzo Trimarchi**, a noble
building built between the late 17th and early 18th century and owned by the Trimarchi
family. The venue's site is barvitelli.it.

## The Godfather scene

In the scene, shot in front of the bar, Michael (Al Pacino) meets Apollonia's father. Shooting
dates vary by source: 1971 for some, 1972 for others [TO VERIFY]. How much has the venue
changed? Sources are split: for some it has stayed frozen in the 1970s, for others Coppola
dressed it as a bar for the scene. On the name, sources don't explain its origin: they tie it
to the character Vitelli.

## Hours, prices, what to order

- **Hours:** an aggregator (updated November 2025) shows 9–17:30, closed Monday [TO VERIFY on
  the official site].
- **What to order:** **granita** (lemon, almond, mulberry) with brioche and local sweets, under the
  pergola.
- **Prices:** reviewers find it touristy and expensive (€7 for a small cappuccino, per one
  review): treat it as a stop for the atmosphere.
- **Inside:** photos and memorabilia from the shoot, in a small free display; there is no separate
  museum.

## Beyond the bar: the church and the village

In Savoca, several sources name the **Church of Santa Lucia** (sometimes called San Nicolò) as the
church of Michael and Apollonia's wedding. The 14th-century facade of the **Church of San Nicolò**
appears in many shots. Tours also cite **Forza d'Agrò** for the scene of Michael's arrival:
[TO VERIFY]. The medieval village looks over the Ionian coast and, on clear days, Etna.

## How to get to Savoca

Savoca is about 21 km from **Taormina**, a little over half an hour by car. A car is the most
comfortable option; by public transport from Taormina take a bus to Santa Teresa di Riva and from
there a local bus (Jonica Trasporti) to Savoca, or a taxi for the last stretch.
